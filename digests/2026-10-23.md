First edition since Oct 16 — a catch-up covering the week's biggest moves: OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol preview, Anthropic's Claude Duet agent app, DeepSeek's V4.1-Flash refresh, an AI-found PHP RCE emergency, and a Kimi K3.1 teaser.

## 🔥 Top Stories

- **OpenAI previews GPT-5.6 Sol, its new flagship, alongside Terra and Luna variants.** The preview post positions Sol as the strongest coding/science/cyber model yet, shipping with a new safety stack detailed in a companion deployments-safety page. Sources: [preview announcement](https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol) · [safety/deployment notes](https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-5-6).

Sources: [DeepSeek news post](https://www.deepseek.com/en/news/deepseek-v4-1-flash/) · [roundup](https://tech-insider.org/deepseek-v4-1-flash-1017-deepswe-search-api-2026/). Leaderboard and hub echoes in the sections below.

Reported as discovered with frontier AI models. Sources: [overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PHP2Shell) · [Oct 22 AI-news roundup](https://killtheai.com/news/top-10-ai-news-today-october-22-2026/).

Still a teaser, not a launch. Source: [analysis of what is confirmed](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/kimi-k3-1-teaser-video-kimi-code-terminal-three-js-release-what-is-confirmed-2026).

## 📄 New Research Papers

Fresh Oct 21–22 arXiv submissions (cs.AI/cs.LG/cs.CL/cs.CV), none overlapping prior editions:

- **Sensorimotor Alignment: A Platonic Proxy for Embodied Policy Success** — cheap offline proxy for robot-policy evaluation instead of closed-loop rollouts. [2610.31670](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31670)
- **A Fix for Softmax and Cross-Entropy** — documents two numerical bugs (Softmax Collapse, Softmax Cancellation) in standard implementations. [2610.31642](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31642)
- **Low-Bit N-Gram Embeddings for Language Models** — shrinks memory-heavy n-gram embedding tables in open-weight LMs. [2610.31646](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31646)
- **Questioning Instrumental Convergence in RLVR: Checkpoint Evidence from OLMo-3** — tests power-seeking/deception emergence claims against RLVR checkpoint data; safety-relevant. [2610.31624](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31624)
- **Causal Chain of Thought: Mitigating Attention Leakage via Context Isolation** — makes CoT steps causally drive answers by blocking dense attention leakage. [2610.31639](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31639)
- **Adaptive Skill Granularity for Coding Agents: Runtime Routing and Macro-to-Micro Recovery** — packages and repairs reusable coding-agent skills at execution time. [2610.31461](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31461)
- **AgentPhishing: A Benchmark of LLM Email Agents Under Adaptive Attack** — adversarial phishing benchmark for computer-use/email agents. [2610.31404](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31404)
- **RIDAR: Safeguarding Research Integrity in Autonomous Research Agents** — detects and intervenes on validity-threatening behaviors in AI-scientist pipelines. [2610.31372](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31372)
- **WeVisOne-VL: Unified Visual Perception from Objects to Pixels** — one VLM for object-level and pixel-level perception via structured next-token prediction. [2610.31329](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31329)
- **PLE: Physical-Engineering's Last Exam** — hard new benchmark for frontier models on multi-hour physical-engineering agentic work. [2610.30537](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30537)
