4. **PaperGym: Rubric-Centered Evolution for Research-Plan Generation** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.31119](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31119)) — Uses rubric-based evolutionary search to generate stronger research plans from LLMs.
5. **When Does Bigger Help? A Controlled Study of LLM Scale for Ontology Learning** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.31118](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31118)) — Carefully measures how much model scale actually helps ontology-learning tasks.
6. **Configurable Semantic Chunking for Biomedical Information Extraction in Retrieval-Augmented Generation** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.31139](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31139)) — A configurable semantic-chunking approach for biomedical RAG information extraction.
7. **Agentic research is oxymoronic** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.31161](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31161)) — A critical position piece arguing the framing of "agentic research" is contradictory.
8. **BRF-GS: Hyperspectral Bidirectional Reflectance Factor Modeling and Image Generation based on 3D Gaussian Splatting** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.31159](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31159)) — Extends 3D Gaussian Splatting to hyperspectral BRF modeling and image generation.
9. **Constant Individual Regret in General Games** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.31166](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31166)) — A theory result on achieving constant individual regret in general games.
10. **Sharp Approximation Rates for Neural Networks with Affine Latent Parameterizations** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.31157](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31157)) — Sharper approximation-rate bounds for networks with affine latent parameterizations.
