# AI Daily Digest — 2026-11-25

Protein design, exoplanet hunting, Australia's duty of care and Google's MCP mandate lead today's news.

## 🔥 Top Stories

**OpenAI says GPT-6 designs proteins from reasoning and code alone — a partner lab built 322 designs, 35 of which bound their target.**
The preprint, *GPT-6 as a Universal Foundation Model for Protein Biology: Beyond Specialized Models*, claims GPT-6 designs structures and sequences without calling a specialised biology model — just reasoning and general-purpose coding tools ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-models-demonstrate-intrinsic-protein-design-capabilities/)). Partner Onava ran the assays:

- **Minibinders** — 35 of 322 (10.9%) bound across five targets: 18 IL-17A, 8 VEGF-A, 5 PD-L1, 3 IL-7Rα, 1 TrkA. SolubleMPNN plus filters still supplied sequences.
- **Sequence design** — sequence withheld: 75 of 88 bound MDM2 (85%), 26 of 42 VEGF-A (62%), 8 of 59 PD-1 (14%); 14 of 107 (13.1%) on three unpublished shapes.
- **Antibody loops** — 128 of 182 VHH loops (70%) kept IL-4Rα binding; 179 of 272 fragments (66%) kept PD-1/PD-L1.
- **Docking** — 93% of scored PoseBusters predictions matched the experimental pose (internal 97%, DiffDock-L 50%); 92% on Scarpino with a known bond in the prompt.
- **Motif scaffolding** — 43.7% passed both criteria on 30 MotifBench challenges vs 27.8% for RFdiffusion; 18.9% vs 17.1% for Proteina on 200 CATH folds.

**The weakness is published too:** complete structure prediction fails — 0.26 mean TM-score against 0.93 for ESMFold2 — and GPT-6.2's 3D designs scored slightly below RFdiffusion computationally. Antibody docking improved from ~0.05 DockQ at GPT-5.6 to 0.57 at GPT-6.2 and 0.81 internal.

**Two amateur astronomers ran Codex and Astra over public NASA archives and produced planet candidates now in front of professionals.**
Kevin Bond, an OpenAI engineer, pointed Codex at ~100,000 Kepler stars: 200+ flagged signals, including 10 already-confirmed planets as a sanity check. Two stand out — KIC 5689121 in Lyra (~13.8-day dip, consistent with a planet ~2.4× Earth's diameter) and faint star KIC 6691030 (4.8-day dip, smaller than Neptune). A SETI Institute astronomer ran an independent search and found both. Ethan Byrd of Primitive ran Astra over ~19 million stars: 49 candidates, one under review with NASA TESS researchers, two more awaiting submission ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/searching-for-distant-planets-with-astra/)). A candidate is not a discovery; confirmation can take years.

**Australia's Digital Duty of Care passes the Senate with a private right to sue and fines up to 5% of global revenue.**
The Senate advanced the Online Safety Amendment (Digital Duty of Care) Bill on 24 November after the government accepted Greens amendments raising maximum penalties to 5% of global turnover and giving individuals the right to sue for harm ([Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/24/social-media-big-tech-fines-global-revenue-australia-digital-duty-care-laws)). Coverage reaches, in the bill's own words, a service that **generates material using artificial intelligence** — not just large platforms. A twelve-month transition follows, with annual risk assessments kept six years.

**Google makes Workspace MCP servers, the Workspace CLI and Cloud Agent Gateway generally available — and requires agents to use them.**
The Workspace AI Tools Addendum requires agents to reach Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Calendar, Chat and Tasks through the MCP servers or the CLI, not the old REST APIs, enforced from **1 February 2027**; agents still calling REST are capped at Standard-tier quotas regardless of the tier their developer bought ([Google Workspace](https://workspace.google.com/blog/product-announcements/built-for-trust-and-control-new-ai-agent-protections-and-developer-tools-in-google-workspace)). Gateways ship with DLP checks, human-in-the-loop rules for sensitive actions, and default-deny outbound rules with indirect-prompt-injection screening. Usage-quota billing starts 1 April 2027.

**Anthropic lets Claude artifacts install as standalone Mac and Windows apps.**
"Install as app" began rolling out in beta on 24 November: an artifact gets its own icon and window, and can use system notifications, menu bar items and connectors without packaging or API keys ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/install-as-app)). Apps still need a signed-in Claude Desktop session, count against account usage limits, and machine access is gated by permission prompts — up to full computer access if granted.

## 📄 New Research Papers

arXiv's newest announced batch is 24 November.

- **ShellBench: real-world agent tasks from 10M anonymised interactions** — 100 tasks, 53,100 graded runs, 12 models, 5 harnesses. GPT-6 Astra tops completion at 62.5% but only 48.8% on all three attempts; 15.7% of runs failed a safety check. Open-source harnesses ahead: OpenClaw 45.23%, Pi 45.22%, Hermes 45.11%, Codex CLI 45.04%, Claude Code 42.87%. [arXiv:2611.32946](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32946)
- **OmniAct: One Policy, One Million Embodiments** — one policy adapting to each body's capabilities, not just transferring task knowledge. [arXiv:2611.33158](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33158)
- **A Bias in Disguise: Tiny Gradient Edits Stop a Small Language Model's Thinking Loops** — Qwen3.5-0.8B closes its think block on 1–2 of 500 MATH-500 answers at a 4,096-token budget; a tiny gradient edit fixes the loop. [arXiv:2611.33133](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33133)
- **Reinforcement Learning Can Be Recovered From Its Own Rollouts** — rollouts are normally discarded after each update; this asks whether the knowledge can be kept. [arXiv:2611.33102](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33102)
- **SAG-Mem-1.0 Technical Report** — long-term conversational memory that must retrieve intermediate evidence only weakly similar to the question. [arXiv:2611.33124](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33124)
- **AIChipBench: Benchmarking LLM Agents for Automated AI Accelerator Design** — a wrong state update can leave the current token correct while corrupting later output, so a design can pass output tests and still be wrong. [arXiv:2611.33093](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33093)
- **U-Statistics-Powered LLM Reasoning** — GRPO has many variants, but group size, a crucial hyperparameter, has had no principled treatment. [arXiv:2611.33064](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33064)
- **Auditing the Judge: Human-Derived Bias Discovery, Quantification, and Mitigation in LLM Judges** — systematic bias in automated evaluators that conflicts with human preference. [arXiv:2611.33065](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33065)
- **FOL-Proof: Verifying LLM Text-to-Logic Translations via Deterministic Semantics** — a semantically unfaithful first-order-logic formula silently poisons reasoning built on it. [arXiv:2611.33078](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33078)
- **Lost on Campus: Evaluating Embodied Spatial Reasoning of Vision-Language Models in the Wild** — spatial reasoning is still mostly evaluated on static images and synthetic scenes. [arXiv:2611.33145](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33145)
- **Worth the Wait? How Response Time and Streaming Shape Judgments of Generative AI Answers** — blind tests on how deliberation versus instant cache hits shape perceived quality. [arXiv:2611.33050](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33050)
- **BioLoom: Scaling Biomedical Vision-Language Models by Learning from the Structure of Scientific Articles** — article structure, not more image-text pairs, as the scaling axis. [arXiv:2611.33151](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33151)
