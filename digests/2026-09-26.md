## 🔥 Top Stories

**Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic plan joint self-regulatory body SAFA, targeting launch by end of 2026 or early 2027.**
- First reported by The Information on Sep 24 and confirmed in announcements Sep 25, the Standards Authority for Frontier AI (SAFA) would set common safety standards for the most advanced models.
- First joint standards body from the three leading frontier labs, landing amid rising global regulatory pressure.
- Sources: [GovInfoSecurity](https://www.govinfosecurity.com/google-openai-anthropic-plan-frontier-ai-standards-body-a-32926) · [GuruFocus](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/9097076/google-openai-anthropic-form-new-ai-selfregulation-body) · [NewsBytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/science/google-openai-and-anthropic-launch-frontier-ai-standard-agency/tldr) · [The420](https://the420.in/google-openai-anthropic-frontier-ai-safety-standards-safa/)

**OpenEvidence raised $250M at a $15B valuation — 25% above its January $12B mark — and is reportedly weighing a sale.**
- Reported by Axios on Sep 25; Business Insider notes backing from hospital systems alongside Andreessen Horowitz interest, with deal chatter including compute-access angles and potential M&A.
- Described as the most widely used AI platform by doctors in America.
- Sources: [RoboticsMedia](https://theroboticsmedia.com/article/openevidence-250m-15b-valuation-a16z-september-25-2026) · [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/chatgpt-for-medicine-openevidence-fielding-valuation-2026-9?op=1) · [AIWeekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/openevidence-raises-250m-at-15b-25-above-januarys-12b-mark-and-weighing-sale) · [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260121029132/en/OpenEvidence-Raises-$250-Million-to-Build-Medical-Superintelligence-for-Doctors)

- Adds native audio support, video tracking, web search, sub-agent calls, and more complex visual reasoning. Listed on OpenRouter Sep 25 as `perceptron/perceptron-mk1.5`.

## 🚀 Frontier Model Releases

**TypeSafe Jev Router (`typesafe/jev-router`) — "System One" model for fast, typed, probabilistic routing decisions.**
- Listed on OpenRouter Sep 25. Routes each request by deciding whether a cheap fast model can answer or the large model is needed, returning constrained answers with probabilities.
- Sources: [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai) · [Preporato Labs](https://preporato.com/labs/jev-model-router) · [awesome-jev-1](https://github.com/bladedevoff/awesome-jev-1)
