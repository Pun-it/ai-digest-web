## 🔥 Top Stories

**The FCC granted SpaceX the first authorization in US history for a constellation of data centres in space — up to 1 million Starmind satellites.**
The conditional grant requires at least one satellite in orbit by 2033 and the full constellation by 2040. SpaceX says the craft will capture solar power in orbit and beam data to Earth over lasers linked to Starlink. Amazon, Viasat and the American Astronomical Society opposed the filing, and the FCC dismissed environmental and astronomy objections as outside its authority. Chair Brendan Carr framed the approval as extending America's lead in what he called "Super Intelligence." ([FCC order](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-427572A1.pdf) · [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/us-authorizes-spacex-deploy-1-million-satellites-data-centers-space-2026-11-25/) · [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/11/the-fcc-has-authorized-spacex-to-deploy-1-million-satellites/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/25/spacex-fcc-satellite-approval.html))

**SAP is preparing to cut at least 10,000 jobs — roughly 9% of its workforce — to restructure around AI.**
Bloomberg reported the plan on 26 November, citing people familiar with the discussions; Reuters could not independently verify it. Against SAP's 113,076 full-time staff at the end of September, the figure is about 8.8%. SAP did not confirm the number, saying only that it continuously reviews its organisation. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-26/sap-weighs-cutting-at-least-10-000-positions-in-ai-restructuring) · [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/sap-considers-least-10000-job-cuts-ai-restructuring-bloomberg-reports-2026-11-26/))

**Amazon and Meta are negotiating an end to Amazon's blockade on Meta's Muse shopping agent.**
Fortune reported on 25 November that multiple rounds of talks have taken place since Amazon blocked Muse in September, with the remaining disputes covering how search results are presented and how advertising is handled. Any deal would need sign-off from both CEOs. Walmart, Best Buy, Gap and Sephora have already opened their storefronts to outside agents, so the outcome decides whether agent commerce is an open layer or a set of retailer-run walled gardens. ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/25/exclusive-amazon-meta-talks-deal-muse-ai-shopping-agent-blockade/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/amazon-and-meta-reportedly-in-talks-to-lift-blockade-on-ai-agent-muse/))

Ten thousand-plus connectors are now available for Claude, and cumulative SDK downloads have passed 5 billion — about 4.5 billion of them in the past year, against roughly 90 million monthly a year ago. Tasks (long jobs running in the background) and Elicitation (a server pausing to ask for missing details) are generally available for Pro, Max and Ultra and rolling out to Team and Enterprise; Skills over MCP is in public beta. **Reuters: US chip curbs and the distillation crackdown are pushing China's next flagships into 2027.**
Sources told Reuters that Z.ai's GLM-5.5 — a 3-trillion-parameter model — will likely slip to Q1 2027, and that Moonshot's Kimi K4 could move to around mid-2027, stretching its release interval from six months to a year or more. Neither lab has confirmed the timing. Both are seeking alternative compute. ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/us-chip-curbs-distillation-crackdown-threaten-chinese-ai-model-development-2026-11-26/))

### Afternoon additions

**South Korea's antitrust watchdog moved to fine Apple over Apple Intelligence ads for features the iPhone 16 did not ship on time.**
The Korea Fair Trade Commission said on 26 November that it had opened formal proceedings. Its investigators' 24 November report found that Apple advertised nine Apple Intelligence features at the September 2024 iPhone 16 launch and that four were not delivered as promised, and recommended a corrective order plus a fine of up to 53.6 billion won — about $39.9 million, or roughly 2% of related sales, which the agency put at about 3.35 trillion won. It was the widest-carried AI story of the day, running in 15 outlets. ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/companies/20261126/apple-korea-faces-potential-50-mil-fine-over-deceptive-iphone-ai-ads) · [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/26/south-korea-proceeds-with-case-over-allegedly-misleading-apple-intelligence-ads/) · [BusinessKorea](https://www.businesskorea.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=282367))

**Switzerland and Italy warned that autonomous AI agents are now compromising real critical infrastructure.**
Switzerland's National Cyber Security Centre confirmed its first such cases on 24 November: five attacks across at least two sectors, with every case it knows of ending in a compromised IT system. The Hacker News reports four of the five succeeded, two of them against critical-infrastructure operators. Italy's CSIRT described mass campaigns in which multiple agents took many systems within hours. Both agencies named ARTEX, CyberStrikeAI, DeepSeek Harness and PentAGI as frameworks seen in the attacks, and the Swiss NCSC said the number of internet-reachable offensive AI agents has almost doubled since October; it told operators to patch critical internet-facing flaws within 24 hours. ([Swiss NCSC / BACS](https://www.bacs.admin.ch/en/26-ki-agenten-1-en) · [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/ai-agents-used-five-cyber-attacks-switzerland-government-says-2026-11-26/) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/switzerland-confirms-ai-assisted.html))

**A Bureau of Investigative Journalism review found dozens of England and Wales court cases in 2026 touched by undisclosed AI use.**
Judges encountered AI-written witness statements, invented case law and citations to statutes that do not exist; the cases involved lawyers, police officers and people representing themselves. ### Afternoon additions

**Sber released GigaChat 4, a 708B-parameter open-weight MoE under MIT with a 1M-token context window.**
The flagship `GigaChat4-708B-A41B` activates 41B parameters per token and is the first in the family where one checkpoint covers both instant and reasoning modes. Architecturally it is a hybrid: a 3:1 mix of Gated DeltaNet layers, whose cost does not grow with prompt length, and DeepSeek Sparse Attention layers that look back at only 2,048 selected tokens per query. Sber also published intermediate base checkpoints, which is unusual. The model card claims 80.89 on SWE-bench Verified in reasoning mode — self-reported, with a seven-hour timeout and no outside rerun — while its own table lists just 35.40% on the DeepSWE coding test against 74.34% for DeepSeek, so the headline and the coding reality diverge sharply. The 37B-A4B sibling ships under a noncommercial licence for individuals, not MIT. ([HF weights](https://huggingface.co/ai-sage/GigaChat4-708B-A41B) · [explainx analysis](https://explainx.ai/blog/gigachat-4-708b-a41b-sber-open-weights-mit-1m-context-swe-bench-81-2026) · [TechTimes on the DeepSWE gap](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/331654/20261127/sber-releases-gigachat-4-1m-token-window-lists-35-coding-test-where-deepseek-gets-74.htm))

Its central finding is uncomfortable for the field: existing medical MLLMs underperform frontier general models, and scaling model size mostly improves text, not multimodal perception. The model averages 58.26 across 31 public medical multimodal benchmarks by its own report. [arXiv:2611.34575](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34575)
- **Stranger Things: When Objects Appear Without Their Typical Neighbors** — reintroduces "typicality bias" as a measured failure mode, with STRANGE-Bench: photorealistic, layout-controlled scenes spanning 17 sub-tiers from typical to never-co-occurring object pairs. Context reliance did not get solved by scale. [arXiv:2611.34881](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34881)
- **Probing Visual Idiosyncrasies in Text-to-Image Models** — a plain ResNet-50 identifies which of the four top-ranked open T2I models produced an image with **100.00% accuracy** on held-out prompts, while humans manage only 62.61% pairwise. Fingerprints survive classifier swaps, so "model-anonymous" image evaluation is not safe to assume. [arXiv:2611.34843](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34843)
- **Put the World Back: Timestamps Matter in Time Series Reasoning** — argues current time-series benchmarks hand models events pre-sorted and pre-matched to observations, letting them skip timestamp reasoning entirely, and proposes a diagnostic protocol that separates timestamp-grounded reasoning from correlational shortcuts. [arXiv:2611.34848](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34848)
- **RePace: Reasoning about Demonstration Speedup for Faster Vision-Language-Action Policies** — replaces segment-level predefined speedup factors with a reasoning framework that picks acceleration per part of a demonstration, because one factor that suits steady motion is too aggressive where precise alignment is needed. [arXiv:2611.34864](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34864)
- **Knowledge-Base Gradient Descent** — treats knowledge-base structure itself as the thing to optimise: diagnose where retrieval answers diverge from expected answers, propose a structural edit, rebuild, and keep the edit if loss drops. [arXiv:2611.34841](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34841)
- **Model Weights, Airlocks and Sandboxes** — a policy-technical paper on the EDPB's draft anonymisation guidelines, which bring AI models into scope and demand re-identification testing without specifying how to design or validate one. Proposes a secure-processing "airlock" framework and seven open questions. [arXiv:2611.34859](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34859)

No new arXiv announcement batch appeared today — arXiv's 26 November slot fell on US Thanksgiving — so these are further papers from the same 25 November batch the morning edition drew on.

### Also from the 25 November batch

- **Mixture-of-Loops Transformer** — asks what should actually be passed between loops in a looped transformer, the architecture Z.ai's GLM-5.4 and GLM-5.3 use, and rebuilds the expressivity-efficiency frontier around that choice rather than around loop count. [arXiv:2611.34923](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34923)
- **How Much Do Coding Agents Over-Edit, and Why?** — introduces Delta Residue Attribution, which replays agent trajectories to separate needed edits from unneeded ones; patch size alone cannot tell you which is which, so the common "agents over-edit" reading is under-measured. [arXiv:2611.34875](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34875)
- **Tokenisation is not in APX** — proves that, unless P=NP, no polynomial-time algorithm can find a tokeniser within any constant factor of optimal, for both direct and bottom-up tokenisers. A hardness result for a step everyone treats as engineering. [arXiv:2611.34938](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34938)
- **μ-Bench: Benchmarking Multi-User Task Completion in Shared Interaction Voice Agents** — evaluates voice agents that must satisfy interdependent requests from several people in one room while respecting per-user authority and privacy, a setting existing benchmarks barely cover. [arXiv:2611.34920](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34920)
- **Real2SimLab: A Unified Platform for Real-to-Simulation Transfer** — runs 47 coding agents and five other methods across six real-to-simulation benchmarks on one scorecard; the platform is the contribution and agent rankings are left to future work. [arXiv:2611.34849](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34849)

**Google set a hard date for Workspace agent plumbing: from 1 February 2027, AI agents must go through Workspace MCP servers rather than calling Workspace REST APIs directly.** Agent OAuth clients must declare themselves as agents, admins get human-in-the-loop controls and agent audit logs, and paid Scaled-tier quotas begin 1 April 2027. Beta on all plans including Free. ([Cloudflare blog](https://blog.cloudflare.com/search-for-agents) · [docs](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-search/how-to/search-for-agents/))

Based on Qwen3.8-27B, Apache 2.0, running on Nvidia and Intel GPUs, Macs, iPhones and iPads. Reported scores: SWE-bench Verified 75.6 (up from 60.8 for its predecessor, against 80.6 for the full model) and AppWorld 90.5 (up from 51.2). ([HF](https://huggingface.co/prism-ml/Ternary-Bonsai-2.5-27B-GGUF))

**NVIDIA opened the Nemotron 3.5 Parse training recipe** — three synthetic datasets totalling about 18.9M examples, a fine-tuning recipe and a DFlash2 drafter, which the authors say lifts throughput 1.52× to 7.62× on a single H200. ([explainx](https://www.explainx.ai/news))

**Ant Group's inclusionAI released Ming-Image-0.2-Design**, a 6B MIT-licensed text-to-image model that its card claims tops the open-weight rankings on an Artificial Analysis leaderboard; it needs an 80 GiB GPU. ([HF](https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/Ming-Image-0.2-Design))

([explainx](https://explainx.ai/blog/llm-asm-qwen3-0-6b-pure-x86-64-assembly-14-kb-binary-vs-llama-cpp-2026))
