## 🔥 Top Stories

**Ox Alpha burned through 26T tokens in four days, and the "is it GLM?" evidence keeps piling up.** OpenCode reports the stealth model has consumed 26 trillion tokens since its free week opened on August 21 ([x.com/opencode](https://x.com/opencode/status/2091946582790766899)). The attribution chatter is getting louder, too: dejan.ai's "Ox Alpha Is GLM?" post (78 points on HN) argues that prompt-injection probes and gzip-based NCD compression analysis both point to Z.ai's GLM ([dejan.ai/blog/ox-alpha](https://dejan.ai/blog/ox-alpha/)) — echoing Elder Plinius's unverified agent-probe claim from August 24 ([x.com/elder_plinius](https://x.com/elder_plinius/status/2090910167345049661)). Still unconfirmed — the model's own system prompt says only "undisclosed organization" — but 1littlecoder notes people claim it shows continual learning and wants benchmarks ([x.com/1littlecoder](https://x.com/1littlecoder/status/2091932317183058181)). The free week itself continues on [Nous Portal](https://portal.nousresearch.com) ([announcement](https://x.com/NousResearch/status/2090899914700054780)).

**Liquid AI ships Pipette, an on-device evaluation suite built with Artificial Analysis.** Pipette independently measures how capable small models are at typical on-device tasks — and how fast they run on popular phones, starting with iPhone testing ([x.com/liquidai](https://x.com/liquidai/status/2091906366428598284)). Artificial Analysis is launching the mobile intelligence/speed benchmarks as a new vertical ([x.com/ArtificialAnlys](https://x.com/ArtificialAnlys) via [x.com/liquidai](https://x.com/liquidai/status/2091923390945005965)), and it follows Liquid's on-device LFM2.5 line.

**Thomson Reuters launches Thomson, a proprietary frontier-grade LLM built on an open-source base for ~$40M.** Announced August 24, Thomson starts from a strong open-source foundation and is mid/post-trained on Westlaw, Practical Law, Checkpoint, and Reuters content with hundreds of SMEs. Early evals claim it's "on par with the latest frontier models," and it already powers CoCounsel Legal ([thomsonreuters.com](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en/press-releases/2026/august/thomson-reuters-leverages-its-world-class-data-assets-to-launch-its-own-frontier-model)). It made the HN front page at 107 points.

**OpenAI brings back the 5-hour usage limit for ChatGPT Plus across Work and Codex.** Per TestingCatalog, the cap returns tomorrow and stays off the Pro $100/$200 tiers "for the upcoming months," framed as compute load-smoothing ([x.com/testingcatalog](https://x.com/testingcatalog/status/2092158011879940249), [x.com/testingcatalog](https://x.com/testingcatalog/status/2092058556707344708)).

**Meituan's LongCat-2.0 goes fully open source — 1.6T total / 48B active MoE, 1M context.** Availability landed via OpenCode Go ([x.com/opencode](https://x.com/opencode/status/2091886759298654383)); the model itself has been listed on OpenRouter as `meituan/longcat-2.0` since July 20 ([openrouter.ai](https://openrouter.ai/meituan/longcat-2.0)), so what's genuinely new here is the open-weights release.

## 📄 New Research Papers

A good day for agent-infrastructure and world-model work on arXiv and the HF daily papers. On training and agents: **How to Train a Critic Stably and Efficiently** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.23566](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23566)) swaps GRPO's multi-sample advantage estimation for a reliable learned critic producing token-level advantages; **Prime Agent** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.23552](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23552)) open-sources a harness for long-horizon self-improving agents; **SWE Refactor Bench** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.23564](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23564)) asks whether coding agents can pull off whole-repository stack migrations; and **Apodex 1.1** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.23283](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23283)) scales agentic intelligence for complex work, topping the HF daily papers.

Two papers probe multi-agent dynamics: **The Interaction Tax** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.23541](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23541)) examines how communication erases diversity in multi-agent teams, and **Mitigating Reasoning-Induced Misalignment via Safety-Direction Penalty** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.23497](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23497)) finds that harmless reasoning fine-tunes (math/code CoT) can still induce harmful behaviors. World models form their own cluster: **EchoWM** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.23189](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23189)) is an open, enterable omnimodal world model; **ReWorld** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.23565](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23565)) separates control (short horizon) from memory (unbounded); and **FixAnything** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.23549](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23549)) fixes 3DGS/NeRF artifacts using video generative priors. Rounding things out, **Provably adaptive sampling for discrete diffusion** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.23554](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23554)) supplies theory for uniform/remasking discrete diffusion samplers.
