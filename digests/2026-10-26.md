- **Kimi K3.1 Pro is here — Moonshot's 2.8T open-weight flagship (Oct 23).**
  Mixture-of-experts with ~104B active params, 1M-token context, multimodal (text/image/video in). Artificial Analysis scores it **55 on the Intelligence Index — 4th overall**, 3rd on GDPval-AA (1,846 Elo, +312 over Kimi K3), and the highest score of any model on Terminal-Bench 4.0.
