**Anthropic publishes a Claude-authored proof of VP ≠ VNP — 70 pages plus a 324-file Lean 4 formalization, machine-checked, human-unchecked.**

The `vp-vnp` project in Anthropic's `formal-math` repository claims, for every field, that circuits for the Hamiltonian cycle polynomial — and for the permanent outside characteristic 2 — need more than m^(c (log m)^(1/3)) gates for all large m, and that the same holds for approximating circuits. That makes VP a proper subset of VNP and puts VNP outside the closure of VP; the README is explicit that it "does not settle P vs NP."

Both the paper, titled *VP ≠ VNP over every field* and credited solely to "Claude (Anthropic)," and the formalization were written by AI models, and the README states plainly that "no human mathematician has checked the proof." CI runs a comparator script that re-checks the Lean statements against a separate `Challenge.lean` with an independently written second kernel, nanoda; the build is expected to name no axiom beyond `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. This lands the same month as the Claude-assisted Riemann formalization, and carries the same status: mechanically checkable, not peer reviewed.

**Virginia and Oregon become the first states to sue SpaceX over Grok, alleging it profited from non-consensual sexualised imagery including AI-generated child sexual abuse material.**

AGs Jay Jones (Virginia) and Dan Rayfield (Oregon) filed separately on Monday, in Richmond and Multnomah County, arguing SpaceX violated the Virginia Consumer Protection Act and Oregon's Unlawful Trade Practices Act by failing to safeguard Grok and X while marketing both as generally safe. The company already faces Grok-related suits and probes in the UK, Spain, Ireland, France and California.

**Anthropic launches Claude Ship in beta — agents that review pull requests, fix them, and approve the ones a repo policy file permits.**

Claude Ship bundles Code Review, which is already generally available, with two new beta agents and a new command:

- **PR Steward** — works failing CI checks, merge conflicts and review feedback, then comments after each push with what it changed and how to undo it; deliberately blocked from approving or merging.
- **Claude Approvals** — reads an approval policy stored as a file in the repository — a smarter CODEOWNERS — and can approve routine changes on its own or require human sign-off, weighing Code Review results or natural-language classifiers.
- **`/ship`** — a command in Claude Code and the desktop app that pushes a change, opens the PR, reviews it and works the feedback.

Team and Enterprise only, GitHub only, billed on tokens, and unavailable to organisations on Zero Data Retention, the HIPAA configuration or customer-managed keys. Anthropic recommends starting Approvals in observe-only mode before branch protection depends on it.

## 🚀 Frontier Model Releases

**Ant Group opens two medical models under MIT — LingSante-3.1-flash at 560B and LingSante-3.1-air-VL at 124B.**

- **LingSante-3.1-flash** — hybrid MoE with roughly 25B active parameters per token and a 1M-token context; aimed at complex medical reasoning, evidence review, professional healthcare communication and long multi-stage tasks.
- **LingSante-3.1-air-VL** — activates 5.5B of 124B, takes text and images on a 256K context; its model card lists medical-imaging report assistance, skin-condition assessment, diet and medical reports, evidence retrieval and medical workflow agents.

Both are MIT-licensed on Hugging Face under inclusionAI, and neither ships benchmarks. The announcement also covered Afu, Ant's consumer health service (formerly AQ) — a company-supplied 150M users and nearly 20M consultations a day — plus real-time AI video consultations and AI answers reviewed by human doctors across 16 specialties, with an international version planned for more than 170 countries.

## 📄 New Research Papers

Newest announced batch, 23 November:

- **TrialAgentBench: Evaluating AI Agents for Clinical-Trial Analysis and Long-Horizon Drug-Development Decisions** — the first benchmark for the sequential, expensive-information decisions that define clinical development, where real trials are too costly to use as an eval. [arXiv:2611.30916](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30916)
- **Replay Before You Reply: Second-Look Prompting for Unified Multimodal Encoding** — under causal attention, whichever modality comes first is encoded blind to the other; replaying the input fixes the asymmetry. [arXiv:2611.30909](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30909)
- **Predicting Rare-Mode Collapse in Recursive Generative Training from Single-Generation Calibration** — shows recursive training can hold a rare region's sample count steady while eroding its characteristic core, and predicts the collapse without running full chains. [arXiv:2611.30897](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30897)
- **Speak When You See It: Proactive Task Assistants Should Wait for Evidence, Not the Clock** — finds two benchmarks for proactive physical-task assistants mostly reward knowing *when* rather than seeing *what*: a timer that never looks at the video raised an alarm at 25s and still scored well. [arXiv:2611.30895](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30895)
- **Verifiable On-Policy Distillation** — attacks the sparse-supervision problem in RLVR by giving the sequence-level verifier reward a direct signal on alternative actions. [arXiv:2611.30325](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30325)
- **What Makes Good Data for Training Coding Agents? A Systematic Study on SWE-Gym** — audits coding-agent training tasks for reward-label correctness, where hidden tests are only checked against the reference fix. [arXiv:2611.30207](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30207)
- **PackLoop: Row-Packed Self-Speculative Decoding for Looped Language Models under Concurrency** — looped models pay T sequential passes per token; self-speculation recovers 2–10× for one request but fades under shared-GPU load. [arXiv:2611.30205](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30205)
- **DexLatentWorld: 3D Cross-Embodiment Latent Action World Models for Zero-Shot Robotic Manipulation** — learns latent actions from action-free video so a world model transfers across embodiments. [arXiv:2611.30907](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30907)
- **World Models as Native Latent Action Models** — recovers dynamics from egocentric human video and heterogeneous robot recordings that share no action space. [arXiv:2611.30898](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30898)
- **AI Deactivation Reduces Loneliness and Increases Social Interaction** — a two-week randomised deactivation of 625 heavy US ChatGPT users cut self-reported loneliness and raised in-person contact, with the loneliness effect persisting two weeks after access returned. From Dora Zhao, Robert Kraut, Diyi Yang and colleagues. [arXiv:2611.25263](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25263)
