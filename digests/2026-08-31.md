## 📄 New Research Papers
Fresh picks from arXiv cs.AI | cs.LG | cs.CL | cs.CV, Aug 28 submissions:

1. **Ladders in Chaos: When, How, and Why Test-Time Scaling Improves LLM Machine Translation** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.28496](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28496)) — Analyzes sequential and parallel test-time scaling for LLM MT, mapping which scaling mode helps when.
2. **ContextPilot: Teaching Agents for Proactive Context Management via Fine-grained RL** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.28476](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28476)) — Trains long-horizon agents to iteratively retrieve and maintain dispersed context with fine-grained RL.
3. **Sliding-window beats linear attention** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.28444](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28444)) — Challenges the linear-attention trend, showing sliding-window attention can win on memory/efficiency/capability.
4. **Blind Men and the Elephant: Probing the Epistemic Myopia of LLMs under Long-Tail Divergent Knowledge** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.28478](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28478)) — Tests whether LLMs retain divergent/contested accounts rather than collapsing to a single canonical answer.
5. **A Formal Limitation on Learning Human Language From Textual Corpora** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.28560](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28560)) — An information-theoretic result on whether a listener can recover speaker meaning from utterance form alone.
6. **When Robots Mishear Us: Mapping the Safety Risks of Voice-Controlled Embodied AI** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.28518](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28518)) — Shows ASR errors in user input can cascade into unsafe outputs from embodied-AI models.
7. **REPLICANT: Learning Policies for Evading and Hardening Malware Detectors** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.28499](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28499)) — Evaluates ML malware-detector robustness against highly capable evasive attackers, and hardens them.
8. **NL2AGBench: Benchmarking LLM Auto-Formalization for AlphaGeometry** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.28481](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28481)) — Benchmarks LLMs converting natural-language geometry problems into formal languages for AlphaGeometry.
