## 🔥 Top Stories

**The European Commission designated ChatGPT as the first standalone AI service under the DSA rulebook, also upgrading Reddit and Roblox.** Each crossed the 45M-EU-user threshold for systemic-risk designation — ChatGPT reported 159M MAU, Reddit 57.2M, Roblox 48M. All three must complete systemic-risk assessments, submit to independent audits, and share data with regulators, with fines of up to 6% of global revenue. ChatGPT is the first individual AI service brought under direct Commission supervision, while Reddit and Roblox were named Very Large Online Platforms. ([euronews.com](https://www.euronews.com/next/2026/08/31/eu-places-chatgpt-reddit-and-roblox-under-strictest-digital-safety-rules))

**The EU AI Office sent its first formal AI Act enforcement RFIs to OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google.** EVP Henna Virkkunen confirmed the requests four weeks after GPAI obligations became enforceable on Aug 2. The RFIs run on two tracks: security/evaluation/monitoring, and training-content-summary compliance. Non-response or misleading answers can carry fines up to €15M or 3% of global turnover — the first real enforcement teeth of the AI Act. ([tokenstead.ai](https://tokenstead.ai/guides/eu-ai-act-first-enforcement-security-rfis))

**FSC Chair Andrew Bailey warns G20 frontier AI could destabilize global finance.** In a letter ahead of the Asheville meeting, the Bank of England governor and FSB chair flagged frontier models' "increasingly sophisticated autonomy and problem-solving abilities, as well as threat capabilities" as a systemic risk through cross-jurisdictional cyber disruption, and separately warned that 'AI-related optimism' plus elevated leverage could amplify a market correction. ([fsb.org](https://www.fsb.org/2026/08/fsb-chair-warns-of-risks-arising-from-frontier-artificial-intelligence-ai-models/))

([chinamoneynetwork.com](https://www.chinamoneynetwork.com/2026/08/29/deepseek-nears-7-4-billion-funding-round-at-74-billion-valuation-ahead-of-2027-ipo)) · ([techstartups.com](https://techstartups.com/2026/08/28/deepseek-nears-7-4-billion-funding-round-at-74-billion-valuation-ahead-of-2027-ipo/))

1. **Ladders in Chaos: When, How, and Why Test-Time Scaling Improves LLM Machine Translation** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.28496](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28496)) — Maps which mode of test-time scaling (sequential vs parallel) helps LLM MT when.
2. **Video Generative Models as Geometry Learner** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.28549](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28549)) — Probes whether video-generation models implicitly learn 3D geometry from image/video data.
3. **Logos: An Agent Harness on a Cross-Process Bus** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.28553](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28553)) — A lightweight agent harness architecture built on a cross-process message bus for multi-agent control.
4. **DARTS: Decoder-Aware Representation Tuning via Surgery for Model Merging** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.28547](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28547)) — A decoder-aware surgery method to merge models more faithfully.
5. **A Formal Limitation on Learning Human Language From Textual Corpora** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.28560](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28560)) — An information-theoretic result on whether a listener can recover speaker meaning from utterance form alone.
6. **When Robots Mishear Us: Mapping the Safety Risks of Voice-Controlled Embodied AI** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.28518](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28518)) — ASR errors in user input can cascade into unsafe outputs from embodied-AI models.
7. **REPLICANT: Learning Policies for Evading and Hardening Malware Detectors** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.28499](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28499)) — Evaluates and hardens ML malware detectors against highly capable evasive attackers.
8. **Training Communication-Efficient Mixture-of-Experts Language Models with Layer Re-Configuration** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.28511](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28511)) — Cuts MoE communication via layer re-configuration while preserving quality.
9. **ContextPilot: Teaching Agents for Proactive Context Management via Fine-grained RL** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.28476](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28476)) — Trains long-horizon agents to iteratively retrieve and maintain dispersed context with fine-grained RL.
10. **NL2AGBench: Benchmarking LLM Auto-Formalization for AlphaGeometry** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.28481](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28481)) — Benchmarks LLMs converting natural-language geometry problems into formal languages for AlphaGeometry.
