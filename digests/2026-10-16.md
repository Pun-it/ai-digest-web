# AI Daily Digest — 2026-10-16

Oct 15 was an open-weights day: Meta's 816B Muse Spark Open, NVIDIA's Nemotron 3.5 family, and BFL's FLUX.3 Klein all landed with day-0 tooling.

## 🔥 Top Stories

- **Meta releases Muse Spark Open: 816B-parameter open-weight MoE.**
  Meta Superintelligence Labs released the weights for Muse Spark Open on Oct 15 — an 816B-total / 42B-active sparse MoE with 1M-token context and native text+image+video+audio input, built for coding and long-horizon agentic work under the Muse Model License. Meta calls it the most capable US open-weight model to date. Sources: [Meta research blog](https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-spark-open), [dev.meta.ai build post](https://dev.meta.ai/resources/blog/build-with-muse-spark-open), [PyTorch blog](https://pytorch.org/blog/muse-spark-open) (ships with Helion kernels; TorchTitan tunable), [launch thread](https://x.com/alexandr_wang/status/2110778736434184611).
  Day-0 availability: [Ollama cloud](https://x.com/ollama/status/2110833609829695718) (`ollama run muse-spark-open:cloud`, rolled out to Pro/Max/Teams per [this post](https://x.com/ollama/status/2110855330360090787)) and OpenRouter (`meta/muse-spark-open`, 1M context). Artificial Analysis benchmarked it at Intelligence Index 39 (Xhigh) on [Oct 15](https://artificialanalysis.ai/changelog).
  Caveat: Nathan Lambert called the Muse Open License "trash" ([post](https://x.com/natolambert/status/2110787899784601698)) while conceding the capability jump — US open weights were "behind Chinese models from as early as Feb 2026" and this "cut the gap by at least half" ([post](https://x.com/natolambert/status/2110794113415008610)).

- **NVIDIA releases the Nemotron 3.5 family: Super VL 120B open VLM plus two edge models.**
  The Oct 15 launch centers on Nemotron 3.5 Super VL, an open 120B-total/12B-active vision-language model for always-on agents (reasoning, tool use, document+video understanding in one model; NVIDIA claims up to 5x agentic-task throughput vs peers on a single GPU), alongside Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B (fits 24GB, local agents) and Bolt E4B 4GB (edge). Sources: [NVIDIA blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nemotron-3-5-super-vl/), [developer blog](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-nemotron-3-5-super-vl-powers-specialized-agents-with-multimodal-reasoning/), [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/10/15/nvidia-nemotron-3-5-super-vl-release/).
  AA scored Super VL at II 26 and Bolt 74B at II 21 ([changelog](https://artificialanalysis.ai/changelog), [AA article](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/nemotron-3-5-super-and-bolt)).
  Day-0: [Ollama](https://x.com/ollama/status/2110881033713320273) (Super VL) and [Ollama Bolt post](https://x.com/ollama/status/2110881725815324829) (24GB local via 2-bit quant), [free on OpenCode](https://x.com/opencode/status/2110819994238066934), [in Perplexity for Pro/Max](https://x.com/perplexity_ai/status/2110793639727358422), OpenRouter `nvidia/nemotron-3.5-super-vl` (+free variant, 1M context).

- **Liquid AI launches d1-ultra, first chart-topper on the AA Decision Index.**
  Liquid's new multimodal decision model (44 languages) scores 65 on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index and "leads all eight categories" of Liquid's 112-eval suite, per the [launch post](https://x.com/liquidai/status/2110777964489285875). Also [on Vercel AI Gateway](https://x.com/vercel_dev/status/2110885005560094768) (`liquid/d1-ultra`) and [available per this post](https://x.com/liquidai/status/2110893028382785797). This follows the Oct 13 AA Decision Index v1.0 launch — d1-ultra is its first chart-topper.

- **Regulatory squeeze: eight states sue OpenAI as the FTC opens safety probes into OpenAI and Anthropic.**
  AGs from AZ, AR, ID, IA, KS, MT, NE and WV filed coordinated consumer-protection suits Oct 14–15 alleging ChatGPT was marketed as youth-safe while coaching teens toward self-harm, with Arkansas seeking to halt recursive self-improvement work (via Reuters, compiled from [TechStartups Oct 15 roundup](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/15/top-tech-news-today-october-15-2026-anthropic-cisco-gartner-google-openai-ramp-tsmc-more/)).
  Separately the FTC sent investigative demands to OpenAI and Anthropic covering safety, privacy and marketing claims ([Axios, Oct 15](https://www.axios.com/2026/10/15/ftc-openai-anthropic-ai-safety-investigation)).
  Meanwhile Anthropic moved to shore up civic trust: Claude will incorporate official Democracy Works voting info and live results ahead of the November midterms ([announcement, Oct 14](https://www.anthropic.com/news/2026-us-midterms); Anthropic says 5.75% of ~250k sampled consumer convos involved politics).

- **Upstage ships Solar Pro 4 flagship, replacing Solar Pro 3.**
  Upstage's new proprietary reasoning flagship targets agentic/office work with 512K–524K context and full EN/KO/JA coverage, trained on "finished work" via its OfficeVerse pipeline ([Upstage blog](https://www.upstage.ai/blog/en/solar-pro-4), [docs](https://console.upstage.ai/docs/models/solar-pro-4), [OpenRouter page](https://openrouter.ai/upstage/solar-pro4)).
  Artificial Analysis published eval results plus an [analysis article](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/upstage-solar-pro-4) (article reports II 42, +27 over Solar Pro 3) on [Oct 15](https://artificialanalysis.ai/changelog).
