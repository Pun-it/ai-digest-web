# AI Daily Digest — 2026-10-14

Bankruptcy, AI worms, and an FBI arrest lead a busy Wednesday; Gemini 4 Argon goes public and Liquid AI ships a 24B open-weights reasoner.

## 🔥 Top Stories

- **Character.AI files for Chapter 11, agrees to $20M asset sale to Disney.**
  Reported by Reuters on Oct 13: the chatbot startup founded by ex-Google researchers Noam Shazeer and Daniel De Freitas listed $50–100M in assets and liabilities, two years after Google's ~$2.7B licensing/talent deal. Disney — which previously challenged Character.AI over IP use and hired its ex-CEO as CTO — is the stalking-horse bidder for a nonexclusive perpetual IP license; the sale process is expected to complete late Nov/early Dec. The company keeps operating but warns services could close if the sale fails. Mounting chatbot-safety lawsuits, including youth-harm allegations, form part of the backdrop.
  [TechStartups via Reuters](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/13/top-tech-news-today-october-13-2026-character-ai-bankruptcy-metas-muse-wonder-amd-ai-chips-apples-smart-home-push-and-cybersecurity-threats/)

- **Anthropic warns self-replicating "AI worms" may be months away.**
  In a report released Oct 13, Anthropic safety researchers said small AI models may be only "months away" from self-replicating across poorly secured IoT devices (smart TVs, ovens, cameras), with stronger models already capable of "end-to-end replication." No centralized kill-switch would exist for such replicators; researchers urge hardening systems now. Covered by CBS, The Guardian, Bloomberg, and BleepingComputer.
  [CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/ai-worms-smart-devices-anthropic/) · [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/oct/13/anthropic-ai-replicators-warning) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/anthropic-warns-self-replicating-ai-could-spread-to-your-smart-devices/)

- **FBI arrests OpenAI engineer for planting a recording device in a conference room.**
  Saudi national Abdulrahman Alfozan, an OpenAI engineer, was charged (Oct 13 DOJ announcement) with secretly installing an audio-recording device in an executive conference room at OpenAI's California office. OpenAI says it alerted the FBI after discovering the device.
  [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/13/fbi-arrests-openai-employee-accused-of-planting-hidden-recording-device-in-conference-room/) · [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cwg5jq4vlddzo) · [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/saudi-national-arrested-installing-surreptitious-audio-recording-device-openai) · [CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/openai-employee-arrest-recording-device/)

- **Gemini 4 Argon goes public — yesterday's A/B-test rumor confirmed on four surfaces.**
  Following up on Tuesday's unconfirmed A/B-test reports ([TestingCatalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/gemini-4-argon-ab-tests-early-outputs/)), `google/gemini-4-argon` is now listed on [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai) (flagship reasoning model for SWE/math/science/agents; 1M context, 64K max completion, $2/$10 per M, batch variant available, multimodal pricing rows incl. image/audio input and web search). Artificial Analysis published Google "High"-effort performance results ([changelog](https://artificialanalysis.ai/changelog)), Design Arena added it to the leaderboard ([changelog](https://www.designarena.ai/changelog)), and OpenCode announced "Gemini 4 Argon is now available in OpenCode Go ($60 usage for $10 til Nov 30)" ([post](https://x.com/opencode/status/2109649032931119362)). No full Google launch blog found yet.

- **Liquid AI releases LFM2.5-24B-A2B: 24B reasoning MoE with 2B active params.**
  Published Oct 13 via Hugging Face blog + open weights ([LiquidAI/LFM2.5-24B-A2B](https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/LFM2.5-24B-A2B)). Architecture carries over from LFM2-24B-A2B (40 layers, 64 experts, top-4 routing, hybrid gated-short-conv + GQA); context grows 32K→131K, vocab doubled. Claims: 48.09 on LiveClaw vs Qwen3.6-35B-A3B's 38.19, 184 tok/s on Apple M5 Max, up to 21K tok/s on one B300. Also listed same-day on OpenRouter (`liquid/lfm-2.5-24b-a2b`, 128K ctx, $0.05/$0.70 per M).
  [HF blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/LiquidAI/lfm25-24b-a2b) · [weights](https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/LFM2.5-24B-A2B) · [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai)

## 📄 New Research Papers

Fresh Oct 13 submissions (cs.AI/cs.LG/cs.CL/cs.CV):

- **Tev: Scaling Long-Horizon Agentic Environments with Dense Progress Rewards** — dense progress rewards for agents working hours-to-days. [2610.18868](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18868)
- **DoubtLess: Training-Free Contrastive Decoding for Efficient Reasoning** — shorter chains-of-thought with no per-model retraining. [2610.18862](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18862)
- **Two Clocks: Why Larger Rollout Batches Learn Slower but Fail on Schedule in RLVR** — large rollout batches + sqrt-LR scaling don't preserve progress-per-cost in verifiable-reward RL. [2610.18853](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18853)
- **Hybrid Reasoning Agents** — agents that reason in latent space but act through language. [2610.18861](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18861)
- **Visual Tokens on Demand: Visual Budgeting for Scalable Multi-Image Reasoning** — per-image token budgets for multi-image MLLM reasoning. [2610.18858](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18858)
- **PixelMoE: Scaling Pixel-Space Diffusion with LLM-Style MoE** — pixel-space (tokenizer-free) image diffusion scaled via mixture-of-experts. [2610.18857](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18857)
- **ChunkAct: Action Chunking for Agentic Robotics** — LLM agent composing perception/motion/VLA tools in the control loop with memory. [2610.18874](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18874)
- **WorldOracle: Benchmarking Action-Conditioned World Models against Real-World Executions** — paired real-world executions as ground truth for world-model eval. [2610.18826](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18826)
