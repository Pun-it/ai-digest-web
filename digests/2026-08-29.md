# AI Daily Digest — 2026-08-29

The flagship open-weights drop that was pending in yesterday's digest finally landed: Z.ai shipped GLM-5.3's full weights, and the OpenAI–Cursor split headlines the business news.

## 🔥 Top Stories

**Z.ai releases the full GLM-5.3 open weights.** The open-weights release that had been pending in yesterday's digest is now live: Z.ai posted on Aug 28 that "GLM-5.3 is now open-weight," and the model is downloadable on Hugging Face as `zai-org/GLM-5.3`, with a technical write-up at z.ai/blog/glm-5.3. The ~753B model reuses the GLM-5.2 base with all gains coming from post-training; Z.ai calls it its "most capable model for agentic coding and cyber defense," citing a completed roughly two-week safety review, a CyberGym score of 84.5, and a vulnerability disclosure ledger covering 2,436 real vulnerabilities. It was showing as HF's #1 trending text-generation model. GLM 5.3 and 5.3-Flash have also fully rolled onto Ollama Cloud (US/EU hosted, zero data retention, Aug 28), and GLM 5.3 is available in Perplexity's Computer, where Perplexity says it beats GLM 5.2 on the WANDR benchmark. ([x.com/Zai_org/status/2093354097122455713](https://x.com/Zai_org/status/2093354097122455713)) · ([huggingface.co/zai-org/GLM-5.3](https://huggingface.co/zai-org/GLM-5.3)) · ([z.ai/blog/glm-5.3](https://z.ai/blog/glm-5.3)) · ([x.com/ollama/status/2093492205029908752](https://x.com/ollama/status/2093492205029908752)) · ([x.com/perplexity_ai/status/2093410587338575936](https://x.com/perplexity_ai/status/2093410587338575936))

**OpenAI is ending its Cursor partnership — model access ends Nov 12.** OpenAI announced it is terminating its partnership with Cursor; the coding tool's access to OpenAI models ends on Nov 12, following Cursor's recent acquisition by SpaceX AI. Cursor has been adding non-OpenAI alternatives (Qwen3.8-Max, Hy4) in recent weeks as the joint venture diverged from OpenAI. ## 📄 New Research Papers

Fresh picks from arXiv cs.AI | cs.LG | cs.CL | cs.CV, Aug 27 submissions:

1. **CLAP: Cross-Embodiment Video World Models are Zero-Shot Physical Simulators** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.27406](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27406)) — Shows action-conditioned video world models can act as zero-shot physical simulators across robot embodiments without per-robot training data.
2. **Boosting LLM Exploration via Weak-Model Guidance in RLVR** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.27420](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27420)) — RLVR training causes policy entropy collapse; a weak model guides exploration to preserve diversity.
3. **Retrieval Heads Meet Vision: Uncovering How VLMs Locate and Extract Visual Information** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.27417](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27417)) — Interprets specialized "retrieval heads" that route visual evidence in vision-language models.
4. **Consolidating RLVR Capabilities Across Domains: A Deep Dive into Fusion Paradigms** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.27409](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27409)) — Studies how to fuse RL-with-verifiable-rewards training across multiple capabilities without degradation.
5. **Reconstructing Humans and Objects in Interaction using Large Reconstruction Models** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.27407](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27407)) — Large reconstruction models for 3D human-object interaction, relevant to AR/VR and robotics.
6. **Mechanistic Reaction Prediction via Discrete Flow Matching on Graph-Structured Electron Occupation** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.27429](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27429)) — Models chemical reactions as transitions in electron space via discrete flow matching.
7. **Learning a Continuous Sepsis Severity Score Without Hour-by-Hour Supervision** ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.27421](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27421)) — A two-site retrospective learning a continuous sepsis severity score without dense labels.
