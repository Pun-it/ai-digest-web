## 🔥 Top Stories

**Google released Gemini 4 Argon on Sep 30 — the first Gemini 4 model and Google's first flagship since Gemini 3.1 Pro in February.**
Pitched at long-horizon work: software engineering, legal/financial research, cyber defense. Benchmarks per Google: DeepSWE v1.1 77.9% (SOTA), #1 on the Vals Index (GDP-weighted finance/coding/legal/tax), AutomationBench 51.3% (#1), LVBench long-video 91.7% (SOTA), plus leads on Vals Finance Agent v2 and Harvey's Legal Agent Benchmark.
Specs: industry-leading 1M-token **output** limit (up from 64K); intro pricing $2 input / $10 output per M with 95% off cached reads. Internal wins claimed: 40% better quantum-subroutine optimization in minutes, 300+ TiB freed fleet-wide by agent-driven memory optimization, C/C++→Rust migrations up to 800K+ lines (Fuchsia Zircon kernel).
Catch: phased rollout — initially only to trusted cyber defenders via the Fairwind Program, with pre-release access going through the U.S. government's voluntary process. Wider dev/enterprise/consumer access "as soon as possible."
Third-party read: Artificial Analysis scores Argon (High) at Intelligence Index 53 and says it "equals GPT-6 Astra at 60% of the cost per task with discounted prices" — "Google is back as one of the top three labs." LMArena added `gemini-4-argon-high` to Text, Agent, and WebDev arenas on Sep 30 (see Leaderboards). Reaction: [@natolambert](https://x.com/natolambert/status/2105436759496773946) welcomed the surprise ("more frontier labs = good for consumers"), and [@testingcatalog](https://x.com/testingcatalog/status/2105389846382489773) ran a launch thread; the same account speculates an in-development AI Studio "Security review" mode may be Argon-powered (unconfirmed, see Quick Hits).
