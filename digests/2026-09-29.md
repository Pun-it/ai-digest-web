## 🔥 Top Stories

**OpenAI DevDay is today (Sep 29, 10 a.m. PT, Fort Mason, free livestream) — and the day before, OpenAI cancelled the planned October GPT-6.1 Astra release after internal alignment tests.**

- The cancellation notice landed Sep 28: do not treat the keynote as a backdoor 6.1 GA. Same evening, @OpenAI posted a "Get ready." teaser (~7M views), and a16z's David George weighed in on OpenAI distribution ("create customers, not stickier models").
- What to watch: always-on assistant "o" (strong leak, zero confirm), Pro-tier movement or an ultra tier, ChatGPT + Codex + Work unification, Agents API beta→GA milestones, GPT-Live-1 inside consumer surfaces; hardware teaser and brand-new flagship beyond Astra are low-confidence. Rumor-grade only for the "o" agent ([TestingCatalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/openai-to-announce-o-always-on-agent-during-devday/)); November-Anthropic-IPO chatter remains single-source talk.
- Logistics: Fort Mason SF, invite-only ($650), free keynote livestream at 10 a.m. PT.
- Sources: [explainx DevDay expectations (updated Sep 29)](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/openai-devday-september-29-2026-august-2026) · [DevDay event page](https://devday.openai.com/) · [OpenAI DevDay announcement](https://openai.com/index/devday-2026/)

**Anthropic shipped Claude Sonnet 5.5 on Sep 28 — the second model in the Claude 5.5 family, ~30% faster, at unchanged Sonnet 5 pricing ($2/$10 per M).**

- Positioned as the faster, lower-cost complement to Opus 5.5 (which launched Sep 22 alongside promises Sonnet/Haiku 5.5 would follow "in the coming weeks"). Artificial Analysis already benchmarked it: Intelligence Index 56 at Max effort — #2 overall, just 2 points behind Opus 5.5 max, but at the highest output-tokens-per-task AA has measured. Scores scale 36/41/47/52/56 across Low→Max adaptive-reasoning efforts.
- Also new on OpenRouter the same day: `anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5` (+ batch variant); registry now at 460 models, up from 458.
- Sources: [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/anthropic-releases-claude-sonnet-5-5-at-unchanged-sonnet-5-pricing/) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/28/anthropic-debuts-claude-sonnet-5-5-running-30-faster-than-the-previous-generation-ai-model/) · [Thurrott](https://www.thurrott.com/a-i/anthropic/342139/anthropic-releases-claude-sonnet-5-5) · [Claude release notes](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12138966-release-notes) · [AA Sonnet 5.5 article](https://artificialanalysis.ai/changelog) · [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai)

**NVIDIA launched the Open Agent Safety Platform (Sep 28): open-source OpenShell runtime plus a Sentry hardware watchdog that can quarantine rogue agents in milliseconds.**

- OpenShell traces all agent actions and enforces policy on NVIDIA Vera CPUs, and extends to Arm/Intel platforms; Sentry runs out-of-band on BlueField-4 DPUs for continuous in-silicon monitoring, with full-stack governance from testing to deployment across compute and robotics.
- Launch coalition spans Anthropic, Microsoft, Hugging Face, Palantir, CrowdStrike, Cisco, Dell, HPE, JPMorganChase, Perplexity, Salesforce, SAP, Scale AI, ServiceNow, Red Hat, Figure, Palo Alto Networks, and SpaceXAI — plus researchers and public-sector orgs aiming at shared best practices and international cooperation.
- Sources: [NVIDIA newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/open-agent-safety-platform) · [NVIDIA developer blog](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-open-agent-safety-platform-a-reference-for-continuous-in-silicon-agent-monitoring/) · [platform page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/solutions/ai/agent-safety/)

**Florida's AG asked a state court Monday to bar OpenAI from developing new models without outside oversight.**

- AG James Uthmeier filed in Highlands County for sweeping temporary restrictions on OpenAI, Altman, and affiliates — including keeping minors off ChatGPT — while Florida's child-harm lawsuit proceeds. Reuters (via Yahoo), Politico, and TNW all confirm the Sep 28 filing; it is a request, not yet a ruling.
- This lands one week after Australia's PM dressed down Altman over the Medicare-portal delay — and two days before Canberra's Thursday inquiry summoning Altman and Amodei (see Quick Hits).
- Sources: [Reuters via Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/florida-asks-court-bar-openai-144145273.html?fr=sycsrp_catchall) · [Politico](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/09/28/florida-injunction-openai-development-01095061) · [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/florida-openai-temporary-injunction-model-development-uthmeier) · [cybersecuritynews](https://cybersecuritynews.com/florida-ag-seeks-emergency-injunction-on-chatgpt/)

**Manus shipped 2.0 on Sep 28 with Cascade, Studio, Automations, Cloud Computer — and Cue, personal agents that each get their own phone number, email, wallet, and cloud computer.**

- Cascade is a rebuilt execution framework aimed at lower token consumption and operating cost; Studio is a desktop surface; Automations are event-driven; Cue is the standalone consumer app (invite code MEETCUE) where teams of agents collaborate and transact within user-set budgets — ordering food, holding places in line, sending messages, making payments.
- The Information frames Cue as a direct challenge to Meta's Muse — notable because Manus recently separated from Meta after the collapsed $2B sale talks.
- Sources: [gpts24](https://www.gpts24.com/en/news/manus-2-0-launches-cascade-agent-framework-and-cue-a-personal-agent-app-with-a-phone-and-wallet) · [explainx Manus 2.0](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/manus-2-0-studio-cue-cascade-cloud-computer-2026) · [briefs.co](https://www.briefs.co/news/manus-rolls-out-manus-2-0-and-cue-app-pushing-personal-ai-ag/) · [The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/manus-unveils-new-personal-agent-app-challenge-metas-muse)
