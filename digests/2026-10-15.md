Note: per third-party analysis, most figures outside the launch post are secondhand/unreplicated.

## 📄 New Research Papers

Fresh Oct 14 submissions (cs.AI/cs.LG/cs.CL/cs.CV), none overlapping the Oct 13 batch:

- **Your Transformer Is Secretly Elastic** — extracting multiple fixed-size submodels from one trained transformer for heterogeneous hardware. [2610.20588](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20588)
- **Continuum Attention** — replaces discrete KV lookup with integration over a continuous memory curve. [2610.20586](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20586)
- **Co-Evolving Acting and Forecasting for Language Agents** — dense intermediate supervision to cut long-horizon rollout costs in agentic RL. [2610.20597](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20597)
- **Latent Compaction with Test-Time Training** — compressing KV caches while preserving unseen-question info. [2610.20582](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20582)
- **One Task, Many Interfaces** — cross-interface consistency training for LLM-agent robustness to action renames/notation changes. [2610.20576](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20576)
- **VASPO: Self-Distillation with Privileged Value Estimation** — dense supervision for reasoning models via a privileged self-teacher. [2610.20516](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20516)
- **Player-1: Learning to Play from Synthetic Experience** — computer-use/game agents from synthetic experience. [2610.20584](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20584)
- **DynThinker: Unifying Static 3D and Dynamic 4D Spatial Reasoning** — one model for both, where 3D post-training previously hurt dynamic understanding. [2610.20598](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20598)
