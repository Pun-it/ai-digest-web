## 🔥 Top Stories

**Mistral launched Large 4 ("Le Chonk") in public preview: 1T-parameter natively multimodal MoE with 49B active parameters; open weights land end of October.**
Per the [official post](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4/) (Oct 6): ML4 is Mistral's largest model to date, served on the Mistral Studio preview API today, trained from scratch on 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs in Mistral's own European datacenters. Mistral claims SOTA-among-open-models on enterprise workloads (cybersecurity, finance, law) and says visual grounding beats even frontier closed models — vendor benchmarks, treat as unconfirmed until independent evals land. Artificial Analysis already scored the preview at Intelligence Index 38 with a [dedicated article](https://artificialanalysis.ai) ("France home to the most intelligent model outside the US and China"). Until weights ship, red-teaming runs with cybersecurity leaders, vetted partners, and state authorities on a reduced-moderation variant. Priced at $1.36/M input tokens per explainx coverage ([writeup](https://explainx.ai/blog/mistral-large-4-le-chonk-1t-open-weights-preview-2026), [docs](https://docs.mistral.ai/models/mistral-large-4-0), [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/mistral-debuts-large-4-le-chonk-a-1-trillion-parameter-text-output-model-with-high-benchmarks-planned-for-open-weights-release)).

**Google shipped Nano Banana 2.1: new Gemini image model live on OpenRouter, debuting atop LMArena's image leaderboard.**
`google/gemini-nano-banana-2.1` appeared on [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai) Oct 6 and `gemini-nano-banana-2.1` joined the [LMArena leaderboard](https://arena.ai/company/leaderboard-changelog) the same day — the first new image-model entry there since grok-imagine-image-2.0 on Oct 2. Nano Banana is Google's Gemini image-generation/editing line ([overview](https://gemini.google/overview/image-generation/)); full specs and pricing TBD pending Google's announcement and independent arena scores.

**DeepSeek is nearing a ~$12B pre-IPO round backed by Tencent and CATL, targeting a listing in early 2027.**
Per [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/deepseek-nears-12b-round-with-tencent-catl-before-2027-ipo) (Bloomberg signal, Oct 6): the lab sought ~50B yuan and, on signed term sheets, may approach 100B yuan (~$12B+), earmarked for compute, model dev, and hiring. Two Chinese labs in two days have now telegraphed landmark 2027 listings funded by domestic strategics — the China cohort looks set to hit public markets with deep war chests.

**The Pentagon told the BBC it has stopped using Anthropic's Claude — though BBC sources say Claude was still used last week in Iran operations via Palantir's Maven.**
Per [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/pentagon-tells-bbc-it-has-stopped-using-anthropics-claude) (BBC signal, Oct 6): a DoD official says the Pentagon ceased Anthropic products months after a national-security supply-chain designation; BBC sources contradict this, saying Claude ran in military operations against Iran via Palantir's Maven Smart System as recently as last week. Backstory: Anthropic refused to lift guardrails on autonomous weapons/mass surveillance, sued the administration, and lost on appeal in September. This prices the cost of the red-line stance for every frontier lab.

**Anthropic expanded its Cyber Verification Program into three tiers (Oct 6), folding in Project Glasswing.**
Per [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/cyber-verification-program): Defense Access (SOC/IR, malware reverse-engineering, vuln validation), Red Team Access, and Specialized Access tiers give vetted security orgs reduced-blocking access to the most capable models — explicitly including Claude Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Claude Mythos 5.1, and future models. Two new model names surface here: Claude Mythos 5.1 and Claude Fable 5.1. Writeup: [explainx](https://explainx.ai/blog/anthropic-cyber-verification-program-three-tiers-glasswing-2026).
