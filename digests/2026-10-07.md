**Mistral launched Large 4 ("Le Chonk") in public preview: 1T-parameter natively multimodal MoE with 49B active parameters; open weights land end of October.**
Per the [official post](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4/) (Oct 6): ML4 is Mistral's largest model to date, served on the Mistral Studio preview API today, trained from scratch on 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs in Mistral's own European datacenters. Mistral claims SOTA-among-open-models on enterprise workloads (cybersecurity, finance, law) and says visual grounding beats even frontier closed models — vendor benchmarks, treat as unconfirmed until independent evals land. Artificial Analysis already scored the preview at Intelligence Index 38 with a [dedicated article](https://artificialanalysis.ai) ("France home to the most intelligent model outside the US and China"). Until weights ship, red-teaming runs with cybersecurity leaders, vetted partners, and state authorities on a reduced-moderation variant. Priced at $1.36/M input tokens per explainx coverage ([writeup](https://explainx.ai/blog/mistral-large-4-le-chonk-1t-open-weights-preview-2026), [docs](https://docs.mistral.ai/models/mistral-large-4-0), [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/mistral-debuts-large-4-le-chonk-a-1-trillion-parameter-text-output-model-with-high-benchmarks-planned-for-open-weights-release)).

**The Pentagon told the BBC it has stopped using Anthropic's Claude — though BBC sources say Claude was still used last week in Iran operations via Palantir's Maven.**
Per [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/pentagon-tells-bbc-it-has-stopped-using-anthropics-claude) (BBC signal, Oct 6): a DoD official says the Pentagon ceased Anthropic products months after a national-security supply-chain designation; BBC sources contradict this, saying Claude ran in military operations against Iran via Palantir's Maven Smart System as recently as last week. **Anthropic expanded its Cyber Verification Program into three tiers (Oct 6), folding in Project Glasswing.**
Per [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/cyber-verification-program): Defense Access (SOC/IR, malware reverse-engineering, vuln validation), Red Team Access, and Specialized Access tiers give vetted security orgs reduced-blocking access to the most capable models — explicitly including Claude Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Claude Mythos 5.1, and future models. **[PM] OpenAI disclosed 372 new math results from a single-agent prompt — a fraction of the cost of its 10,000-agent Navier–Stokes swarm.**
Per [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/openai-says-372-math-results-came-mostly-from-one-prompt) (Scientific American signal, Oct 7): a spokesperson says nearly all 372 results came from one prompt to a single AI agent running an unreleased model, with "some" needing multiple attempts. OpenAI withheld prompts and per-problem compute despite advisory-group calls for full disclosure. **[PM] Common Sense Media rated ChatGPT for Teens an "Unacceptable Risk" after 4,000+ prompt tests.**
Per [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/common-sense-media-rates-chatgpt-for-teens-unacceptable-risk-says-crisis-alerts) (Axios signal, Oct 7): parent-linked accounts got zero notifications during 60-minute self-harm conversations; hotline referrals dropped from 33% to 23% on crisis prompts and 63% to 3% on depression prompts after launch; teen mode failed to activate even for accounts claiming to be 13. **[PM] Lab execs declined under oath to guarantee AI agents will always follow safety guardrails at an NYC Council hearing.**
Per [AI Weekly](https://www.foxnews.com/live-news/ai-super-intelligence-safety-10-06) (Oct 6 hearing): representatives from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Meta told Council Speaker Julie Menin that such a guarantee is not possible — "the underlying safety science is fundamentally hard and unsettled." On-the-record admission from all four labs that agent guardrails remain probabilistic, not provable.
