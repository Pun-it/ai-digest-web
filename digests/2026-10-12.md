# AI Daily Digest — 2026-10-12

A quiet release day led by OpenAI context upgrades, Reka's edge VLM, and fresh open image weights.

## 🔥 Top Stories

- **OpenAI raises GPT-6.1 Sol default context to 500k in Codex and ChatGPT Work.**
  Up from 272k (~80% increase), aimed at fewer compactions on large tasks. OpenAI's Tibo S. (@thsottiaux) says the default "will go higher in the future." Day 7 of the ship-a-day sequence.
  [testingcatalog post](https://x.com/testingcatalog/status/2109431898691031316) · [thsottiaux Day 7](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2109430343346581527)

- **GPT-6.1 Sol Ultrafast is generally available at $12/$60 per M tokens.**
  Confirmed GA through the Responses API (rate-limited) after last week's rumor — up to ~8x faster than Sol Standard at 6x standard API prices ($2/$10). Available in API, Codex, and ChatGPT Work; steering corrections in Codex are now instant/real-time.
  [kju.ai](https://kju.ai/story/openai-sets-gpt-6-1-sol-ultrafast-api-pricing) · [runtimewire](https://runtimewire.com/article/openai-gpt-6-1-sol-ultrafast-api-pricing) · [okaynews](https://www.okaynews.com/openai-gpt-6-1-sol-ultrafast-api-pricing-october-9-2026/) · [testingcatalog Oct 9 brief](https://x.com/testingcatalog/status/2108465147186470979)

- **Reka ships Edge 2603: 7B open VLM for physical AI.**
  A 1024×1024 image encodes in 331 input tokens vs ~1063 for Cosmos-Reason2 8B-class models. Positioned for robotics, automotive, and real-time video.
  [LLM Gateway timeline](https://llmgateway.io/timeline) · [Reka Edge page](https://reka.ai/reka-edge) · [AIWeekly alert](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/reka-ships-edge-2603-a-7b-vlm-at-331-tokens-per-1024px-image)

  Details are sparse ("Wild MISSINGNO. appeared!"), usable in Hermes Agent today.
  [MISSINGNO post](https://x.com/NousResearch/status/2109012611555840069)

## 📄 New Research Papers

Oct 9 submissions (arXiv cs.AI/cs.LG/cs.CL/cs.CV):

- **AI Models Can Provably Hide Arbitrary Capabilities** — capability evals may be fundamentally unable to elicit hidden behaviors under adversarial conditions. [arXiv 2610.13928](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13928)
- **RSI-Anything: Unlocking Recursive Self-Improvement with World Models** — turning interaction experience into persistent policy gains without costly external feedback. [arXiv 2610.13912](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13912)
- **Splat2Act: From Captured Scenes to Personalized VLA Manipulation** — personalizing generalist manipulation policies (e.g. π0.5, 0% pickup on unseen home objects) to the user's own scenes/objects. [arXiv 2610.13945](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13945)
- **Can machines reason non-verbally?** — visual non-verbal reasoning gaps persist even in advanced systems. [arXiv 2610.13925](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13925)
- **X-AgentRed: Red-Teaming Personal Agents in Evolving Multi-Service Ecosystems** — harm emerging from accumulated cross-service tool effects. [arXiv 2610.13886](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13886)
- **RATED: Reliability-Aware AI Agent Tool Retrieval via Execution Dynamics** — retrieval that accounts for live tool availability, not just semantic relevance. [arXiv 2610.13889](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13889)
- **Can Language Models Play the Wikipedia Game?** — retrieval over large collections requiring specialist knowledge. [arXiv 2610.13948](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13948)
- **When VLMs Destroy What They Know: Spatial Representations Fall Below Lexical Priors** — VLM internals vs. the spatial structure they claim to reason about. [arXiv 2610.13910](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13910)
- **LapMerge: Differentiable Exact-Budget Token Merging in Linear Time** — token compression without the selection trade-off. [arXiv 2610.13858](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13858)
- **Chronos Policy: Learning Robot Policies on Physical Time** — dropping the fixed-rate sequence assumption for variable sensing/control delays. [arXiv 2610.13929](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13929)
