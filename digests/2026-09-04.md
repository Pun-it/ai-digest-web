**Nvidia agrees to acquire Hugging Face for ~$12.93B — confirmed Sep 3; platform pledged to stay open to rival silicon.** Nvidia filed an 8-K on Sep 2 confirming an ~$11.9B cash purchase plus a ~$1B equity-retention pool for joining employees, expected to close in H1 2027 subject to regulatory review. ## 📄 New Research Papers

Fresh picks from arXiv cs.AI | cs.LG | cs.CL | cs.CV (Sep 2–4 submissions):

1. **Post-Training Language Models for Gold-Medal Performance in Coding Competitions** ([arxiv.org/abs/2609.02849](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02849)) — the Nemotron IOI recipe (22K-problem dataset, synthetic reasoning traces, SFT+RL) driving Nemotron-3-Ultra-CC to 535.4/600 on IOI 2026, above the top human's 498.27.
2. **Principia: Relational Physics Tests for Video Models** ([arxiv.org/abs/2609.04200](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04200)) — a new benchmark for physical reasoning in video models using relational/relative-motion questions that are invariant to frame rate, object scale and camera calibration (Sep 3).
3. **Clean Engineering, Unstable Measurement: A Preregistered Reliability Failure of Black-Box LLM Observers on Shared Endpoints** ([arxiv.org/abs/2609.04198](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04198)) — LLM-as-judge gate training data and drive leaderboards, but this preregistered study finds black-box observer reliability collapses on shared endpoints (Sep 3).
4. **SolarWM: Open Data and Scalable Training for Long-Horizon Video World Models** ([arxiv.org/abs/2609.02886](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02886)) — a fully-open foundation for interactive video world models, from data prep to long-horizon inference.
5. **Discriminative World Models for Web Agents** ([arxiv.org/abs/2609.02885](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02885)) — ranking candidate web-agent actions by predicting resulting web states with a discriminative world model.
6. **A Common Measure of Communication for Speech Brain-Computer Interfaces** ([arxiv.org/abs/2609.02887](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02887)) — a unified metric to compare speech-BCI decoding quality.
7. **Thinking in Pictures: A Systematic Benchmark for Reasoning-driven Image Generation** ([arxiv.org/abs/2609.02864](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02864)) — benchmarks unified generative models and world simulators on reasoning-backed image synthesis.
8. **Rethinking On-Policy Distillation of LLMs II: One Training Example** ([arxiv.org/abs/2609.04172](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04172)) — investigates whether on-policy distillation can be driven by a single teacher example (Sep 3).
9. **User Feedback Provides a Unique Signal that LLMs Cannot Detect** ([arxiv.org/abs/2609.02859](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02859)) — argues naturally-occurring user feedback is an irreplaceable learning signal the model itself can't replicate.
10. **The Implications of Linguistic Illegibility for LLM Security** ([arxiv.org/abs/2609.02852](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02852)) — James Mickens on how a model's "illiterate" internal representations interact with its security surface.
11. **Puffin-World: Scaling a Unified Multimodal Model with Native 3D World States** ([arxiv.org/abs/2609.04196](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04196)) — unified multimodal architecture integrating physical understanding, spatial simulation, and native 3D world generation/reconstruction without external offline modules.
12. **Compile by Training: Turning Natural-Language Specifications into Local Neural Functions** ([arxiv.org/abs/2609.04199](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04199)) — turns natural-language function specs into small local neural implementations, cutting cost/latency/provider dependence for recurring text functions.
13. **Legibility is Not Interpretability: Comparing Judged and Actual Importance in Chain-of-Thought Reasoning** ([arxiv.org/abs/2609.04194](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04194)) — challenges treating legible LLM reasoning traces as true interpretability.
14. **Temporal Self-Distillation: Learning Visual State Tracking in Videos Without Supervision** ([arxiv.org/abs/2609.04203](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04203)) — first fully self-supervised framework for continuous video state tracking (S3T).
15. **Last Translation Benchmark** ([arxiv.org/abs/2609.04173](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04173)) — new benchmark probing the limits of MT models as standard translation benchmarks near saturation.
