## 🔥 Top Stories

- **Nvidia agrees to acquire Hugging Face for $12.93B — confirmed Sep 3, the platform stays open to rival silicon.** Nvidia filed an 8-K on Sep 2 confirming an ~$11.9B cash purchase plus a roughly $1B equity-retention pool for joining employees, expected to close in H1 2027 subject to regulatory review. CEO Jensen Huang's blog frames it as scaling the open-model home (18M+ developers, 3M models, 500K datasets, 200K companies) while pledging Hugging Face will "remain an open platform" — models, frameworks, single-cloud providers and compute won't be required to run on Nvidia. CEO Clement Delangue told CNBC HF approached Huang over the summer because open-source AI "needed more resources, more scale." It is Nvidia's second-largest deal after the Groq asset buy, valuing HF at ~$150M annualized revenue. (See Notable X Posts for security researcher elder_plinius's easter-egg find: the first digits of the price decode to Unicode U+1F917 — the 🤗 emoji.) ([blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-to-acquire-hugging-face](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-to-acquire-hugging-face/)) · ([cnbc.com](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/03/nvidia-agrees-to-buy-hugging-face-for-almost-13-billion-ai-expansion.html)) · ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/business/nvidia-buy-hugging-face-nearly-13-billion-big-bet-open-ai-models-2026-09-03/)) · ([x.com/elder_plinius](https://x.com/elder_plinius/status/2095658583832801765))

- **Google ships Gemini 3.8 Flash — and a defender-only 3.8 Flash Cyber — three weeks after 3.7 Flash, at the exact same price.** Gemini 3.8 Flash went GA Sep 2 priced identically to 3.7 Flash ($0.75/1M input, $3.75/1M output, through Dec 31) with the same 1M-token context and text+image+video+audio+PDF input to text output. Reasoning is on by default at low/medium/high (default medium); there is no Live API and no image/speech output. Independent evaluators score it 59 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index vs 3.7 Flash's 56 — the headline gain is agentic reasoning for long-horizon coding and autonomous agents. 3.8 Flash Cyber, available exclusively to trusted defenders via the new Fairwind Program, reports 70%+ on internal vulnerability discovery across 20 languages, 2.6x more correct patches to Chrome vulnerabilities than leading commercial models, and 47.2% pass@1 on CWE-Bench. ([blog.google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/3-8-flash-and-3-8-flash-cyber/)) · ([deepmind.google/models/gemini/cyber](https://deepmind.google/models/gemini/cyber/)) · ([codersera.com](https://www.codersera.com/blog/gemini-3-8-flash-complete-guide-2026/)) · ([thehackernews.com](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/google-anthropic-and-openai-unveil.html))

- **Meta releases Muse Spark 1.3 for Muse Code and the Meta Model API — ~20% fewer tool calls and 25% fewer tokens than 1.2, with stronger long-horizon agentic work.** Rolling out Sep 2 at $1.25/1M input and $4.25/1M output (1M context), it's billed as improving agentic and coding performance while being easier to use in real-world harnesses: it asks clarifying questions when prompts are ambiguous, confirms before consequential actions, better sustains multiple workflows in one long thread, and adapts to a user's preference for frequent updates vs silent background work. Max reasoning arrives shortly after safety testing; prior reasoning modes are available now. Zuckerberg separately said the "Watermelon" model and Muse Spark open weights are "coming soon." Muse Code and Muse Code in Ollama (`ollama launch muse`) get day-0 support. ([research.meta.ai/blog/introducing-muse-spark-1-3](https://research.meta.ai/blog/introducing-muse-spark-1-3)) · ([axios.com](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/02/meta-debuts-muse-spark-13-as-personal-agent-work-continues)) · ([flowtivity.ai](https://flowtivity.ai/blog/meta-muse-spark-1-3-benchmarks-ai-agents/)) · ([x.com/AIatMeta/status/2095234385129963666](https://x.com/AIatMeta/status/2095234385129963666))

- **Sanders and Casar introduce the "Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act" — banning ASI outright and pausing advanced AI research until Congress sets federal safety rules.** Sen. Bernie Sanders and Rep. Greg Casar's bill would permanently prohibit development of AI that matches or exceeds human capabilities and impose a temporary halt on frontier research until a new cabinet-level federal oversight agency (plus international agreements) is in place. ([sanders.senate.gov](https://www.sanders.senate.gov/)) · ([x.com/elder_plinius/status/1794428769563820222](https://x.com/elder_plinius/status/1794428769563820222)) · ([aiweekly.co/ai-news-today](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today))

- **OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra scores 62.7% on ARC-AGI-3 and reaches OpenAI's "Critical" cybersecurity threshold — with launch limits on cyber capabilities.** ARC Prize's independent evaluation (Sep 3) shows Astra at 62.7% on ARC-AGI-3 Semi-Private with the standard harness (~$26K), jumping to 99.9% with a provider-adapter harness that preserves reasoning state; it used fewer actions than the human baseline on 96% of levels. OpenAI's "Path to Astra" post (Sep 1) says it scored 100% on ExploitBench internally, but OpenAI will constrain its cybersecurity capabilities at launch (a harder internal 20-high-severity-V8-vuln benchmark was also built). ## 📄 New Research Papers

Fresh picks from arXiv cs.AI | cs.LG | cs.CL | cs.CV (Sep 2-3 submissions):

1. **Post-Training Language Models for Gold-Medal Performance in Coding Competitions** ([arxiv.org/abs/2609.02849](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02849)) — the Nemotron IOI recipe (22K-problem dataset, synthetic reasoning traces, SFT+RL) that drove Nemotron-3-Ultra-CC to 535.4/600 on IOI 2026, above the top human's 498.27.
2. **SolarWM: Open Data and Scalable Training for Long-Horizon Video World Models** ([arxiv.org/abs/2609.02886](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02886)) — a fully-open foundation for interactive video world models, from data prep to long-horizon inference.
3. **Discriminative World Models for Web Agents** ([arxiv.org/abs/2609.02885](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02885)) — ranking candidate web-agent actions by predicting resulting web states with a discriminative world model.
4. **A Common Measure of Communication for Speech Brain-Computer Interfaces** ([arxiv.org/abs/2609.02887](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02887)) — a unified communication metric to compare speech-BCI decoding quality.
5. **Thinking in Pictures: A Systematic Benchmark for Reasoning-driven Image Generation** ([arxiv.org/abs/2609.02864](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02864)) — benchmarks unified generative models and world simulators on reasoning-backed image synthesis.
6. **User Feedback Provides a Unique Signal that LLMs Cannot Detect** ([arxiv.org/abs/2609.02859](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02859)) — argues naturally-occurring user feedback is an irreplaceable (non-replicable by the model itself) learning signal.
7. **The Implications of Linguistic Illegibility for LLM Security** ([arxiv.org/abs/2609.02852](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02852)) — James Mickens on how a model's internal "illiterate" representations interact with its security surface.
8. **Debias-SparseGPT** ([huggingface.co](https://huggingface.co/)) — a bias-aware pruning variant that keeps debiased outputs intact for Qwen/Llama under aggressive sparsification.
