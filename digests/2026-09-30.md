## 🔥 Top Stories

**OpenAI DevDay keynote (Sep 29) shipped 20+ announcements headlined by Dots — always-on agents powered by Astra that keep working between conversations.**

- Give a Dot a responsibility (monitoring bug reports, prepping a budget cycle, migrating off a retiring API); it works in its own cloud computer + browser, writes and tests code, and uses 4,000+ connected apps. Onstage demos: gathering product feedback, an app redesign via Codex, joining Slack threads to take delegated tasks.
- ChatGPT Space is the shared home: collaborative Pages/files, Team Tasks, @ChatGPT in Slack/Teams (no individual license needed to participate), macOS Meetings beta, Specialist Dots (Enterprise preview), Microsoft Agent 365 integration. Dots roll to Pro and Business Premium; chats with your Dot don't count toward usage limits.
- Sources: [OpenAI DevDay recap](https://openai.com/index/devday-2026-recap/) · [BenchLM DevDay breakdown](https://benchlm.ai/blog/posts/openai-devday-2026) · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1001681/openai-devday-2026-biggest-news-announcements) · [RuntimeWire](https://runtimewire.com/article/everything-openai-announced-at-the-devday-2026-keynote) · [CNBC live blog](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/29/openai-devday-2026-live-updates.html)

**GPT-6.1 Sol is the new general-purpose model: near-Astra capability at a fifth of Astra's price — and it already replaced GPT-6 Sol after 7 days.**

- Standard API: $2 input / $0.10 cached-input read / $2.50 cache write / $10 output per M (up to 272K tokens). Positioned for coding, computer use, and repeated agent work. On OpenRouter day-one as `openai/gpt-6.1-sol` plus a `pro` reasoning-mode variant, 1050K context.
- Artificial Analysis: Intelligence Index ladder 42/48/50/51/52 across low→max — max just 1 point below Astra at less than a quarter of the cost per task.
- Sources: [OpenAI: Introducing GPT-6.1 Sol](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-1-sol/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-gpt-6-1-sol-says-it-nearly-matches-gpt-6-astra-and-costs-less/) · [AA changelog](https://artificialanalysis.ai/changelog) · [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai)

**Ultrafast is a speed tier, not a model — up to 8× Standard generation (300 tok/s) in Codex, 6× in the API — gated behind a new $500/mo Pro 500 plan.**

- Tiers: Standard 1×, Fast ~2× (2× price), Ultrafast up to 8× (6× price). Launches on Astra first (Sol support coming later) across API, ChatGPT Work, and Codex. Pro 500 offers ~25× Plus usage; Pro 200 reopened at $200/mo with Sol positioned for frequent use.
- Developer platform: Agents API in public beta (managed Codex harness — sessions, orchestration, memory, computer use), Codex Cloud (tasks carry across devices, voice-steered CLI, /agents view), Sign in with ChatGPT for partner apps, fuller plugin interfaces, MCP Events, and an OpenAI Marketplace for buying software. Also previewed: Private Intelligence / Private Inference (fall 2026), 1.2B ChatGPT weekly users reported via coverage.
- Sources: [BenchLM DevDay breakdown](https://benchlm.ai/blog/posts/openai-devday-2026) · [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/openai-devday-2026-dots-gpt-6-1-sol-codex-developer-tools-126093000396_1.html) · [Latent Space AINews](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-openai-devday-2026-dots-61) · [aiagentslibrary recap](https://www.aiagentslibrary.com/blog/openai-devday-2026/)

**Beyond DevDay — two Sep 29 launches worth knowing: Inception's Mercury Voice and Artificial Analysis' local-agent benchmark.**

- Mercury Voice is a diffusion LLM (dLLM) tuned for voice agents: reasons, calls tools, follows long system prompts at conversation-grade latency. Follows the Mercury Coder diffusion line. Source: [Inception: Introducing Mercury Voice](https://www.inceptionlabs.ai/blog/introducing-mercury-voice) (via [BenchLM confirmed releases](https://benchlm.ai/model-updates))
- AA-AgentPerf-Local is AA's open-source tool for benchmarking agentic AI on laptops/workstations, with launch results across DGX Spark, Ryzen AI Halo, MacBook Pro (M5 Pro), and RTX 5090 on four open-weights models. Source: [AA changelog](https://artificialanalysis.ai/changelog)
