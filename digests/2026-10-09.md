# AI Daily Digest — 2026-10-09

Six stories lead: GPT-6's consumer rollout, Grok's budget video model, a $500M Manus raise, Anthropic's cyber push, Microsoft's on-device Windows plan, and AMIE's first real-clinic trial.

## 🔥 Top Stories

- **OpenAI rolls GPT-6 out in ChatGPT with "Intelligent UI."**
  Following up on this week's GPT-6 API coverage, this is the consumer rollout: ChatGPT answers can now render as interactive interfaces (charts, calculators, maps, forms) instead of walls of text. Pro, Business and Enterprise got it first in the Chat tab; Free and Go tiers followed on Oct 8. Applies to the Chat experience, not Work/Codex models. Corroborated by [@sama](https://x.com/sama/status/2107924408597950702) and [@OpenAI](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2107894997538525580).
  [OpenAI announcement](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-for-everyone/) · [ExplainX explainer](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/chatgpt-intelligent-ui-gpt-6-interactive-answers-explained-2026) · [Shattered overview](https://shattered.io/gpt-6-intelligent-ui-chatgpt-rollout-2026/) · [Community thread](https://community.openai.com/t/gpt-6-and-intelligent-ui-in-chatgpt/1404139)

- **xAI launches Grok Imagine Video 1.5 Lite: $0.02/s at 480p, $0.03/s at 720p, $0.14/s at 1080p.**
  Distilled from Grok Imagine Video 1.5 for speed; text-to-video and image-to-video; 1080p rendered at 720p and upscaled. Artificial Analysis placed it 17th on its text-to-video leaderboard the same evening — two spots above Google's Veo 3.1 at roughly a third of the per-second price.
  [ExplainX breakdown](https://explainx.ai/blog/grok-imagine-video-1-5-lite-pricing-artificial-analysis-rank-17-veo-3-1-2026) · [OpenRouter page](https://openrouter.ai/x-ai/grok-imagine-video-1.5-lite) · [CloudPrice](https://cloudprice.net/models/xai-grok-1-5-imagine-video-lite) · [AgentBreaking](https://agentbreaking.com/models/x-ai--grok-imagine-video-1.5-lite/)

- **Manus parent raises $500M+ after Beijing blocked Meta's ~$2B takeover.**
  Butterfly Effect's first round since the Meta unwind, led by Boyu and IDG; revenue run-rate reportedly $400–500M by June 2026. Signals Beijing prefers a capitalized independent Manus over a US acquisition.
  [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/08/manus-fund-raise-meta-muse-tencent.html) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/ai-startup-manus-raises-500m-075135971.html)

- **Anthropic launches "Cyber Mission."**

  [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-cyber-mission) · [CybersecurityNews](https://cybersecuritynews.com/anthropic-cyber-mission/)

- **Microsoft pushes "hybrid intelligence" Windows.**
  MAI-Code-1.1 Flash (137B total / 6.8B active) for local coding, MXC agent sandboxing GA on Windows 11, and Meta Muse coming to Windows.
  [Microsoft command line](https://commandline.microsoft.com/local-models-sandboxed-tools-github-windows/) · [Tech-ish](https://tech-ish.com/2026/10/08/microsoft-wants-windows-pcs-to-build-apps-with-local-ai/)
