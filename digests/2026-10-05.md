**OpenAI's safety team is in open crisis: three researchers fired (Oct 1) and its safety lead quit with a broadside essay (Oct 3).**

## 📄 New Research Papers

Oct 2 batch (IDs 2610.035xx–2610.0371x) — all new vs the Oct 1 batch carried below:

- **4DCodeBench: Benchmarking Agents on Inverse Graphics of Dynamic Scenes** — agents reconstruct dynamic scenes from video as executable graphics programs, incl. re-implementing physical simulations. [abs/2610.03715](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03715)
- **MoSE3: Learning World-Space SE(3) at Every Pixel** — first feed-forward model predicting dense 6-DoF rigid transforms per pixel from monocular RGB video (point tracks only give translation). [abs/2610.03716](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03716)
- **ProAR: Learning Prospective Reasoning with Autoregressive Video Models** — fixes AR video models' short-sighted reactive generation so intermediate states validly reach a target outcome. [abs/2610.03664](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03664)
- **Credit Where It Matters: Dependency-Aware Policy Optimization for Terminal Agents** — traces read-write dependencies between terminal commands for sharper RL credit assignment in coding/debugging agents. [abs/2610.03634](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03634)
- **LESSER: Post-Training Data Selection with Output-Layer Gradients** — ranks post-training data using cheap output-layer gradients instead of full-parameter backward passes. [abs/2610.03702](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03702)
- **Threat-Preserving Representation Sensitivity in Agent-Security Benchmarks** — asks whether ASR scores measure agent security or benchmark representation; introduces TPRS. Timely next to the OpenAI safety fallout. [abs/2610.03585](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03585)
- **HyperBrowseComp: A Multilingual and Multimodal Stress Test for Web-Browsing Agents** — 423 hand-authored, human-validated questions across 13 languages targeting obscure, multi-hop, heterogeneous-source answers. [abs/2610.03574](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03574)
- **Recursive Harness Self-Improvement for Frontier Reasoning Data Synthesis** — task-harness co-evolution: solver failures become reusable skills while the harness itself improves. [abs/2610.03548](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03548)
- **Planning to Learn** — a classifier is a policy with exact, smooth policy gradients — a clean lens on why policy-gradient methods struggle. [abs/2610.03667](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03667)
- **IDRF: Inverse-Distilled Reward Fine-tuning of Masked Discrete Diffusion Models** — reward fine-tuning for few-step masked-diffusion generators via inverse-distillation regularization. [abs/2610.03641](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03641)
