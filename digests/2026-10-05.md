## 🔥 Top Stories

**OpenAI's safety team is in open crisis: three researchers fired (Oct 1) and its safety lead quit with a broadside essay (Oct 3).**
The Wall Street Journal reported Oct 1 that OpenAI fired three safety researchers for allegedly sharing confidential company information with a third-party AI-safety organization. Two days later, safety lead David Robinson — who oversaw the safety reports on 12 frontier launches — quit and published an essay in The Atlantic titled "I Quit OpenAI Because Its Culture Is Broken," arguing the industry's approach to safety "will guarantee more failures unless something changes." Back-to-back blows to OpenAI's safety credibility, one month after the agent-safety scandals and days after the FTC probe opened (as mentioned in the digest of 2026-10-01).
Sources: [WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/openai-parts-ways-with-researchers-who-allegedly-shared-confidential-information-aebac528) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/01/openai-cuts-ties-with-three-safety-researchers-wsj-reports/) · [The Atlantic](https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/2026/10/openai-safety-team-resignation/688881/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/328427/20261002/openai-fires-3-safety-researchers-allegedly-sharing-info-external-safety-groups-says-report.htm)

## 📄 New Research Papers

Latest arXiv batch (Oct 1; IDs 2610.0219x–2610.0221x) — all new vs the Sep 30 batch in the digest of 2026-10-01. No Oct 2–5 batch published yet (weekend). Picks:

- **KaliBench: A Fine-Grained Benchmark for Cybersecurity Tool Use on Kali Linux with Runtime-Free Verifiable Rewards** — measures LLMs' ability to generate executable commands for real security workflows, not just answer quiz questions. [abs/2610.02206](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02206)
- **RPG (Reconstruct, Practice, Go Real): Guided Self-Improvement for Embodied Agents** — autonomous robot skill improvement without updating model weights. [abs/2610.02204](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02204)
- **ScholarCatalyst: A Benchmark for Retrieving Papers That Inspire New Research** — retrieval for taste/inspiration: which prior ideas a new problem actually needs. [abs/2610.02202](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02202)
- **VISTA: A Visual Harness for Reasoning in an Interactive World** — a visual harness giving general multimodal models long-horizon interactive vision across environments. [abs/2610.02200](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02200)
- **TACO: Ternary Absolute-max Column-wise One-sparse Optimizer for LLM Fine-Tuning** — full-parameter fine-tuning with near-zero optimizer-state memory via ternary one-sparse updates. [abs/2610.02199](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02199)
- **Hierarchical Continuous Diffusion Language Models** — fixes the independent-token-sampling bottleneck of discrete diffusion LMs. [abs/2610.02193](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02193)
- **InterEvolve: Test-Time Evolution of Reward Programs for Humanoid Loco-Manipulation** — repurposing existing controller skills for untrained tasks, improving from its own attempts without retraining. [abs/2610.02196](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02196)
- **ROWBench: Do Video Models Render What the Program Specifies?** — checks whether programmable world models visually adhere to explicit rules — a next-gen-game-engine eval. [abs/2610.02205](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02205)
- **NEPA (Embedding Prediction Helps Image Generation)** — predicts the next continuous embedding as the diffusion condition instead of reusing one static prompt embedding. [abs/2610.02203](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02203)
- **FERPO: Forward Entropy-Regularized Policy Optimization** — online RL for continuous control that fixes critic-gradient inaccuracy. [abs/2610.02198](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02198)
