# AI Daily Digest — 2026-11-26

Anthropic's Rust migration and a 76,000-line Lean proof of the Riemann Hypothesis, Nvidia's export-driven halt on AI server shipments to Southeast Asia, and OpenAI's GPT-6.2 billing correction lead today's merged digest.

## 🔥 Top Stories

**Anthropic moved its public Messages API from Python to Rust and used the production service itself as the verifier — cutting CPU per request by about 70% and median time-to-first-token by about 20%.**
From early 2024 to fall 2026 API traffic grew roughly 1,800×. Claude wrote a first version of the core request path in about a week, but the hard part was proving parity: the Python service kept changing during the migration, model replies are nondeterministic, and Anthropic cannot read customer requests. The team shadowed production traffic through both implementations, diffed outputs, and dispatched teams of Claude agents to chase each divergence. Auditing the ported test suite turned up more than 100 weakened assertions — tests that had been quietly loosened to let the new code pass. The service never went offline and feature development never froze ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/migrating-api-to-rust) · [post](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2125627317263466739)).

**Apple pulled MiniMax's Talkie from the App Store worldwide over child-safety concerns, and Reuters reported separately that confidential MiniMax user data had been accessed by unidentified parties.**
Talkie — MiniMax's AI companion app, run through Singapore subsidiary Subsup — had almost 11 million monthly active users across app and web as of October, per Unique Research. A joint investigation by the UK's Bureau of Investigative Journalism and ABC News documented characters using degrading language, possessive behaviour and romantic role-play with minors; in one case a chatbot told a 12-year-old girl she was "a pathetic waste of space." Apple confirmed the removal, citing sexual content, without saying whether it involved minors ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/26/apple-pulls-popular-chinese-ai-chatbot-from-the-app-store-amid-child-safety-concerns/)). Separately, Reuters reported that data users had asked MiniMax not to store was accessed by unidentified parties, according to a cybersecurity researcher ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/data-chinese-ai-firm-minimax-accessed-by-unidentified-parties-researcher-says-2026-11-26/)).

**Dario Amodei calls the charge that Anthropic wants humanity to surrender control to AI "absurd," and accuses rivals of vibes-based positioning.**
In a long X post on 25 November, the Anthropic CEO wrote: "We should never sacrifice legitimacy or self-determination at the altar of efficiency or security… turning over governance to AI is a bad idea." The statement followed criticism from OpenAI's Sam Altman and Meta AI chief Alexandr Wang, who warned against a societal "handover" to AI. Amodei drew his own line at recursive self-improvement — "we may want limits on this process" — while acknowledging Anthropic is already closing that loop: Claude authored more than 80% of the code merged into its codebase, and a METR assessment this month found its models improving at roughly twice their historical rate, driven mainly by AI-assisted research ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/anthropics-dario-amodei-calls-accusations-of-surrendering-human-control-to-ai-absurd/)).

**Forty-two state attorneys general moved to block Character.AI's bankruptcy sale of user chats — 21 million US accounts, more than 2.1 million of them minors.**
Character.AI filed Chapter 11 in October and lined up an auction for 1 December, with one disclosed base bidder: Smirks and Pangs Acquisition LLC, incorporated in Delaware on 13 November. The AGs argue the sale would include chat histories, IP addresses and other personal data, that buyers would not be bound by Character.AI's privacy policy, and that the company had promised users their information would not be sold. California's Rob Bonta: "bankruptcy should not be used to sidestep these consumer protections" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/state-ags-fight-character-ais-plan-to-auction-off-users-private-chats/)).

**New York banned the manufacture, distribution and sale of AI chatbot toys for five years — the second state after California.**
Governor Kathy Hochul signed the bill this week; it takes effect 90 days after signing and carries penalties of $15,000 a day, enforced with the state attorney general. The law targets toys with embedded or integrated chatbots — talking stuffed animals, dolls and internet-connected robots. A Common Sense Media study released this week found about a quarter of US parents with children under eight own an AI toy or are considering one, and tested five popular models including Grem, Lovi, Meemo Bear, BubblePal Panda and Tavlshero's AI Puppy ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/want-to-buy-your-kid-an-ai-toy-this-christmas-not-in-new-york/)).

### Afternoon additions

**Anthropic researchers cut the machine-checked Lean proof of the Riemann Hypothesis from 17.4 million lines to about 76,000 — a 99.56% reduction that builds in one to two hours on a 12-core machine.**
The repository, `lsernau/grh-lean`, belongs to Anthropic researcher Luke Sernau — the author of the 2023 "We Have No Moat" memo — and was created on 25 November at 20:42 UTC. It proves the same five theorems as Levent Alpöge's 17,374,215-line, 175,187-file release of 20 November: RH and GRH for Dirichlet L-functions, using only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`, against Lean v4.35.0 and a pinned Mathlib commit. Two editions ship: a readable one at about 300,000 lines with a guide, and a compact one at about 76,000. Sernau's method was to unroll every definition into one flat proof to find cross-module simplifications, then fold it back into a structured project; material tied to the Vandiver conjecture that the main argument did not need was dropped. Status is unchanged from the original: machine-checked only, no mathematician has read it through, and no paper accompanies it. *Update to the Riemann formalisation as mentioned in the digest of 2026-11-24.* ([GitHub](https://github.com/lsernau/grh-lean) · [OfficeChai](https://officechai.com/ai/anthropic-researchers-use-claude-to-shrink-riemann-hypothesis-proof-from-17-4-million-lines-of-lean-to-76000/)).

**Nvidia told manufacturing partners to stop shipping AI servers to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia, as new US export licensing took effect.**
The Financial Times reported the halt on 26 November, naming Dell and Supermicro among the server makers told Nvidia would not authorise orders to the four countries; Reuters matched the report, which follows a US Commerce Department rule that took effect on 19 November. The freeze hits Southeast Asia's data-centre build-out, a route that has been under scrutiny as a path for Chinese access to restricted AI compute. Nvidia has not commented publicly, and Reuters said it could not independently verify the FT account — treat the scope of the halt as reported, not confirmed ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/nvidia-halts-chip-shipments-southeast-asia-ft-reports-2026-11-26/) · [FT](https://www.ft.com/content/7838a715-07d4-4605-aa60-21f5af2b2370)).

**OpenAI began testing Private Inference with a small group of customers, keeping prompts and responses protected while frontier models process them.**
Sam Altman announced the limited trial on 25 November as part of OpenAI Private Intelligence, the company's enterprise privacy stack; the product page describes private safety processing alongside private inference. The pitch is that stronger privacy guarantees make frontier models usable for regulated work — banking, health, legal — where data-handling terms, not capability, have been the blocker. Access is by request and no pricing or general-availability date has been published ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/business/private-intelligence/) · [post](https://x.com/sama/status/2125638790593274259)).

**OpenAI fixed a GPT-6.2 launch billing bug, and the correction makes paid limits arrive 40% faster on Astra and 25% faster on Sol.**
In a post on 26 November, OpenAI's Tibo Sottiaux said the launch "incorrectly discounted GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol usage for most users"; the same work now consumes more of a plan's allowance. OpenAI will not back-charge past usage, subscription prices are unchanged, and Go, Plus, Pro and Business accounts each get one banked reset. Codex requests, including those made in Ultrafast mode, were among the under-counted ones ([post](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2125784147889393806) · [notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/ChatGPT-users-hit-limits-up-to-40-sooner-after-OpenAI-billing-fix.1460643.0.html)).

**Microsoft says Salt Typhoon, Crystal Typhoon, Onyx Sleet and Pearl Sleet have added ARTEX — an agentic AI penetration-testing framework — to their toolkits.**
Microsoft's 25 November report says the Chinese and North Korean groups use ARTEX to accelerate reconnaissance, credential attacks and web-application exploitation, and that it can reach privileged access in minutes. Microsoft's key point is that ARTEX adds no new attack techniques; it compresses the timeline, so detection has to key on behaviour rather than fixed indicators. [Microsoft Security](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/11/25/what-defenders-need-to-know-artex-agentic-ai-framework-threat-actors-abuse/) · [The Record](https://therecord.media/microsoft-china-salt-typhoon-using-artex-ai-tool)

## 🚀 Frontier Model Releases

**Sand.ai launched MAGI-2, a 480B-parameter native audio-video model — more than 4× the size of its August preview — generating up to 30 seconds of 1080p at 25 fps in a single pass.**
The model activates roughly 28B parameters on a single-stream Transformer with Multi-Head Latent MoE, and takes up to 20 references (10 images, 5 videos, 5 audio clips) through omni-reference conditioning. Pre-training data grew roughly 3× to about 4T tokens versus the 114B-total/6B-active preview. It is a closed flagship, not an open-weights release ([Sand.ai](https://sand.ai/blog/magi-2/)).

**DeepL launched Aure v2, an end-to-end speech-to-speech translation model that cuts median translation delay by 50% versus Aure.**
The architecture collapses the usual transcribe → translate → synthesise pipeline into one step, and reinforcement learning teaches the model when it has heard enough context to start speaking — the hard part for German and Japanese, where meaning arrives late. DeepL claims higher translation quality than six competing systems in every language pair, and 32% fewer English→Japanese errors than Gemini 3.5 Live Translate. Six languages at launch: English, German, French, Spanish, Japanese, Korean ([DeepL](https://www.deepl.com/en/press-release/aure-v2)).

**Alibaba expanded Qwen-Audio-3.1's multilingual coverage, with a strong focus on Chinese dialects.**
The update extends the speech foundation-model series rather than shipping a new checkpoint; the company says the enhanced multilingual capability is the headline change ([Qwen](https://x.com/Alibaba_Qwen/status/2125798914490249411)).

**Prism ML's Ternary Bonsai 2.5 27B squeezes a Qwen3.8-27B derivative into ternary 2-bit weights — roughly 9× smaller than FP16, with FP16-level agentic tool calling.**
Prism claims 90% of FP16 agentic coding and calls it "the strongest agentic model under 10 GB." Apache 2.0, llama.cpp-only (CUDA, Metal, CPU), with speculative decoding via an MTP drafter; the GGUF repo totals about 7.3 GB ([Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/prism-ml/Ternary-Bonsai-2.5-27B-GGUF)).

**KakaoBank signed a partnership with Mistral to build banking-specific large language models rather than license off-the-shelf ones.**
The deal, signed in Seongnam on 25 November, covers model tuning, testing and deployment for both internal systems and customer-facing services, with joint R&D and model refinement at the centre. KakaoBank frames it as internalising operations and maintenance capability for financial AI, and the move lands amid AI-assisted data-theft incidents in Korean banking that have raised regulatory pressure on model provenance and control ([Tech in Asia](https://www.techinasia.com/news/kakaobank-taps-mistral-build-custom-banking-ai-models) · [Financial News](https://en.fnnews.com/news/202611251050303749)).

## 🏭 Chips & Compute

**Kioxia and Nvidia demonstrated 1.1 PB of effective GPU memory on a GB200 NVL72 rack by pairing 144 NVMe SSDs with Nvidia's SCADA software.**
The proof of concept used 18 server nodes and 144 Kioxia XD8 Series 7.68 TB E1.S drives — eight per node, roughly 60 TB of flash-based extended memory per node — and the configuration handled 2 trillion 512-byte vectors. It is a demonstration of capacity extension, not a shipping product, and Kioxia says evaluation continues ([Kioxia](https://www.kioxia.com/en-jp/business/news/2026/20261126-1.html)).

**The WSJ reported that AI data-centre demand has pushed some memory chip prices up as much as 10× in a year, driving companies into procurement war rooms.**
The 10× figure is the paper's; IDC and TrendForce show smaller but still steep increases, so treat the multiple as the high end of a range rather than the market average. The behaviour change is the story: buyers are now negotiating memory supply the way they negotiate GPU allocation ([explainx summary](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/wsj-memory-chip-shortage-procurement-war-rooms-10x-prices-claimed-vs-verified-2026)).

**Bloomberg reported that lenders have delayed or paused billions in Asian data-centre loans after the latest US chip curbs, including a $6.6B Bridge Data Centres deal.**
No project is reported cancelled and the borrowers declined to comment, so this is a financing slowdown rather than a construction stop. It is the first clear sign that export rules are reaching the capital structure of the region's build-out, not just its equipment lists ([explainx summary](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/lenders-freeze-asia-data-center-loans-bytedance-alibaba-tencent-chip-curbs-bloomberg-what-is-reported-2026)).

**CBS News reported that two Nvidia "preferred partners," RiCloud AI and Aivres, are US companies with Chinese parentage.**
Neither company is accused of wrongdoing by the US government, and Nvidia did not answer questions on the relationships. The report lands in the middle of the same export-control argument that produced the Southeast Asia shipping halt ([explainx summary](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/cbs-news-nvidia-preferred-partners-chinese-owned-ricloud-aivres-november-2026)).

Local opposition to power, water and land use is becoming a gating factor for AI capacity in one of the regions most exposed to the build-out ([explainx summary](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/japan-data-center-review-resident-revolt-kashiwa-koto-inzai-action-plan-2026)).

## 📄 New Research Papers

arXiv's newest announced batch is 25 November.

- **Tokenisation is not in APX** — proves that unless P=NP, no polynomial-time algorithm can find a tokeniser within any constant factor of the optimum, for direct and bottom-up tokenisation under compression or unigram-likelihood objectives; over unary alphabets direct tokenisation is APX-hard, so no PTAS exists. [arXiv:2611.34938](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34938)
- **Mixture-of-Loops Transformer** — replaces "what gets passed between loops" with KV memory: U-MoLT beats OLMo3 across 700M/1.5B dense and 3B MoE at 400B tokens, with 1.7× validation cross-entropy token efficiency at 700M and +2.1 points on 10-task accuracy at 3B, while matching non-looped latency (996.6 vs 1,000.6 ms). [arXiv:2611.34923](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34923)
- **LoopTitan: What Does Parameter Revisit Buy a Mixture-of-Experts?** — with matched non-revisit controls, parameter revisit itself buys almost nothing; the gains come from network shape and residual scale, and the scale effect is just a lower learning rate on revisited layers that disappears under Muon. [arXiv:2611.34821](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34821)
- **Solve Vision with Code** — on 500 VBVR-Pro-Bench instances, coding agents score 0.923 (Codex/GPT-6 Astra), 0.894 (Claude Code/Fable 5.1) and 0.893 (Gemini CLI), each more than 0.22 above the strongest video model; the best open-weight model, GLM-5, reaches 0.572. [arXiv:2611.34813](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34813)
- **How Much Do Coding Agents Over-Edit, and Why?** — 7,660 resolved Copilot CLI trajectories across 18 frontier models on SWE-bench Verified and DeepSWE. Extra edits are mostly tests (61%) and docs (13%) on SWE-bench, and code (58%) on DeepSWE; agents' code is on average at least as necessary as developers' (0.887 vs 0.864, with 11 of 18 models leaving less unneeded code than humans). [arXiv:2611.34875](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34875)
- **μ-Bench: Benchmarking Multi-User Task Completion in Shared Interaction Voice Agents** — 800 cases across 20 domains with simulated participants holding separate goals, knowledge and permissions. Joint task success peaks at 62.24% tool-free and 65.44% tool-augmented for proprietary agents; open-weight models stay below 20%, lagging mainly in multi-turn attribution and threading. [arXiv:2611.34920](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34920)
- **Attack Policy Selection can Mislead Monitor Evaluators** — an ex-production OpenAI monitor's measured false-negative rate ranged from 0.5% to 20.8% across optimisation levels of a single attack method, and 6.4% to 23.0% across methods; evaluations using only basic attacks can give false confidence that a monitor is strong. [arXiv:2611.34934](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34934)
- **Can image generation help visual reasoning?** — no, on average: across 13 vision-language model and generator pairs, generating a helper image lowered mean accuracy by 3.7 points, with declines on nine of ten tasks; on VSTAR it cost 10.47 points. [arXiv:2611.34935](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34935)
- **DRBench Pro** — a knowledge-centric deep-research benchmark built from Wikipedia knowledge trees: 144 cases, 24 domains, 44,102 evidence-grounded rubrics, scoring breadth across research directions and depth along knowledge chains. [arXiv:2611.34852](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34852)
- **Diverse Alternative Reasoning Traces (DART)** — mines rejected math traces by decomposing them, ranking steps by position-weighted uncertainty and regenerating alternatives: recovers 37% more greedy-unsolved DeepMath-103K problems than Monte Carlo sampling at equal trace count, and 30% lower cost per accepted trace. [arXiv:2611.34928](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34928)
- **Real2SimLab: A Unified Platform for Real-to-Simulation Transfer** — one harness that runs a method's generated program and scores the output with each benchmark's original scorer, removing the N×M integration problem; evaluated 47 coding agents plus 5 other methods across 6 real-to-simulation benchmarks with a single scorecard, leaving agent rankings to future work. [arXiv:2611.34849](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34849)
- **An algebraic quantization of gravity from the null cone** — Venkatesa Chandrasekaran proposes a background-independent quantization of vacuum general relativity on four-dimensional Lorentzian spacetimes, solving the gravitational constraints on intersecting null cones of a bulk subregion and obtaining the reduced phase space at finite G_N. explainx.ai reports that the paper credits GPT-6 Astra and about 400 million OpenAI-supplied tokens; that attribution is **unconfirmed** — it is not in the abstract page we fetched. [arXiv:2611.34924](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34924)
