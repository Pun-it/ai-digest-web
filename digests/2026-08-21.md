4. **AI4AI-Bench: Benchmarking LLM Agents in Algorithmic Design for Recursive Self-Improvement** — 10 repos ×10 algorithm families, 4h on B300, rerun-from-scratch 0/0.1/1.0 scoring; mean 0.166 across 29 configs — most never change how model learns; reasoning effort lifts changer share 8%→64%. ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.20318](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20318))

5. **Pandora's AI Model Routing Box: Efficient Allocation with Costly Value Estimation** — Formalizes routing to heterogeneous specialists when value estimation itself is costly (Pandora's Box) and analyzes allocation efficiency. ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.20316](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20316))

7. **Phantom Gains: Auditing Self-Improvement Against a Measured Null** — Measured-null audit to separate real self-improvement from phantom gains due to variance/task-order/underspecification. ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.20290](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20290))

8. **Inject, Align, Recover: Staged Post-Training for Retrieval-Free Document Knowledge Internalization** — Staged pipeline internalizes document knowledge without retrieval at inference; recovers performance vs RAG. ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.20281](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20281))

9. **Break It Down, Pass It On: Cross-Task Skill Transfer in LLM Agents** — Decomposes tasks into transferable skills; cross-task transfer improves sample efficiency. ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.20274](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20274))
