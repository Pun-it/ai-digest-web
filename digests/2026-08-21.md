4. **AI4AI-Bench: LLM agents fail to design training algorithms (Aug 20)** — New benchmark asks agents to rewrite the *training algorithm* itself (10 repos, 10 algorithm families, 4h on B300, rerun-from-scratch scoring where 0=uninformative, 0.1=shipped algorithm, 1.0=optimum). Across 29 configs of 6 systems mean score is 0.166, best 0.250 — most submissions never change how the model learns. More reasoning effort raises the share that do from 8% to 64%. Suggests recursive self-improvement via algorithm design remains far from solved. ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.20318](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20318))

5. **ConceptGuard reveals current unlearning fails on dual-use concepts (Aug 20)** — New NeurIPS E&D benchmark constructs complementary forget/retain sets around *dual-use concepts* (harmful vs benign uses of same concept) and evaluates with intent-sensitive, concept-level metrics. Current methods show weak contextual separation and strong forgetting-utility trade-offs — ROUGE and concept metrics both poor. Dataset released. ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.20338](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20338))

## 📄 New Research Papers

From arXiv `cs.CL | cs.AI | cs.LG | cs.CV` sorted by submittedDate descending (fetched 2026-08-21 06:27 UTC). All from Aug 20 — none overlap with digests of 2026-08-18/19/20.

1. **ConceptGuard: Benchmarking Context-Sensitive Unlearning in Large Language Models** — Introduces dual-use concepts where forget/retain sets are complementary in *usage* not just facts; shows current unlearning has weak contextual separation and poor forgetting-utility trade-offs. Dataset released. ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.20338](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20338))

2. **G-CARL: Grounded Checklist-Aligned Reward Learning for Patient-Oriented Medical Report Interpretation** — New PMRI task + MMedReport benchmark requiring factual + accessible explanations of medical reports; checklist-aligned RL with multi-source retrieval for claim verification beats SFT/holistic RL per clinician eval. ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.20331](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20331))

3. **Inducing Task Models from Computer-Use Traces** — TMI discovers latent tasks in interleaved screenshot/action traces and induces hierarchical objective + procedure models; recovers tasks at 0.974 agreement and improves held-out accuracy +30% via derived skills. ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.20319](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20319))

4. **AI4AI-Bench: Benchmarking LLM Agents in Algorithmic Design for Recursive Self-Improvement** — 10 frozen repos × 10 algorithm families; agent gets 4h on B300 to rewrite training algorithm, rerun-from-scratch scored 0/0.1/1.0. Mean 0.166 across 29 configs — most agents never change how the model learns. ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.20318](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20318))

5. **Pandora's AI Model Routing Box: Efficient Allocation with Costly Value Estimation** — Formalizes routing queries to heterogeneous specialists when estimating value is itself costly (Pandora's Box); analysis of allocation efficiency. ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.20316](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20316))

6. **MidTool: Mid-training Data Synthesis for Agentic Tool Use** — Studies synthesizing mid-training data to teach agentic tool-use before post-training; improves tool-use generalization. ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.20314](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20314))

7. **Phantom Gains: Auditing Self-Improvement Against a Measured Null** — Proposes a measured-null audit to distinguish real self-improvement gains from phantom gains due to variance/task-order/underspecification. ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.20290](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20290))

8. **Inject, Align, Recover: Staged Post-Training for Retrieval-Free Document Knowledge Internalization** — Staged pipeline to internalize document knowledge without retrieval at inference; recovers performance vs RAG baselines. ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.20281](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20281))

9. **Break It Down, Pass It On: Cross-Task Skill Transfer in LLM Agents** — Decomposes tasks into transferable skills and shows cross-task skill transfer improves agent sample efficiency. ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.20274](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20274))

10. **Inter-X++: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Multimodal Human-Human Interaction Analysis** — Large benchmark for multimodal human-human interaction (vision/language/motion) with evaluation protocol. ([arxiv.org/abs/2608.20312](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20312))
