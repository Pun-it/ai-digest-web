*The frontier opens up today: Tencent ships a 770B MoE, GLM-5.3's full weights land on HF, and the AI-arena leaderboards reshuffle.* — All links verified Aug 28.

## 🔥 Top Stories

**Tencent releases and open-sources Hy4 preview — a 770B/49B-active MoE with a 1M-token context, the biggest frontier release of the day.** Announced Aug 28, Hy4 preview is Tencent's most capable model yet: 770B total params, 49B active per token, 1M-token context, Apache-2.0 open weights, priced at ~$0.834 per million input tokens. It follows Hy3 (July 6, 295B/21B) and HyCreator; gains are biggest on long-horizon software engineering, document-heavy office work, and scientific research. It landed on OpenRouter as `tencent/hy4-preview` the same morning (created Aug 28, 06:09 UTC). ([hy.tencent.ai](https://hy.tencent.ai/research/hy4-preview?langVersion=en)) · ([github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hy4-preview](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hy4-preview)) · ([tencent.com](https://www.tencent.com/tencent-releases-and-open-sources-tencent-hy4-preview/)) · ([explainx.ai](https://explainx.ai/blog/tencent-hy4-preview-770b-moe-1m-context-august-2026))

**Z.ai's full GLM-5.3 open weights are set to drop on Hugging Face today (Aug 28).** After GLM-5.3-Flash (the former "Ox Alpha") shipped Aug 26, Z.ai confirmed Aug 27 that the full GLM-5.3 weights would be released on HF the following day — the HF page shows an Aug 28 release and the `zai-org/GLM-5.3` repo is staged. ([x.com/Zai_org](https://x.com/Zai_org/status/2092814169263218860)) · ([llmhangar.com](https://llmhangar.com/blog/deploy-glm-5-3))

Elder Plinius re-posted the plea.
