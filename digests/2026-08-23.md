# AI Daily Digest — 2026-08-23

## 🔥 Top Stories

1. **DeepSeek V4 Flash Vision Exp — experimental multimodal agent model lands (Aug 21-22)** — DeepSeek announced `DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Vision-Exp` live on the DeepSeek API Platform: it matches V4-Flash on text (agents, reasoning, world knowledge) and makes a major leap on multimodal agent benchmarks (vision+text). It also appeared as `deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp` on OpenRouter. DeepSeek is pitching it as the vision companion to the Flash line, not a V4-Pro replacement. ([x.com/deepseek_ai](https://x.com/deepseek_ai)) · ([openrouter.ai/deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp](https://openrouter.ai/deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp))

2. **Marin 535B-A23B open training kicks off — 2.7e24 FLOPs on 11× GB200 NVL72 (Aug 21)** — Stanford's Marin project (Percy Liang) started training **Marin 535B-A23B** this week: 535B total / 23B active MoE, with a plan of 80% pretraining plus 20% mid-training on 18.75T tokens over roughly three months, run fully in the open. Nathan Lambert (ex-Olmo co-lead) reposted it, saying he's excited to work with the community on post-training — the longest open MoE run since Olmo scale. ([x.com/natolambert](https://x.com/natolambert)) · ([x.com/percyliang](https://x.com/percyliang))

3. **NVIDIA AVO scores 100% on ARC-AGI-3 — all 183 levels across 25 environments (Aug 21)** — NVIDIA AI reported that its general-purpose coding agent **NVIDIA AVO** completed every level of the interactive ARC-AGI-3 benchmark with no instructions, explicit rules, or stated goals — figuring out objectives purely by interaction. It's a rare perfect score on a benchmark designed to frustrate memorization. ([x.com/NVIDIAAI](https://x.com/NVIDIAAI))

4. **Ox Alpha's free week rolls on, and Muse Spark 1.2 Contributor becomes OpenRouter's newest model (Aug 22-23)** — `stealth/ox-alpha` (1M context, mandatory reasoning at max/high/low, multimodal) remains free through OpenCode (100T tokens/day capacity) and Nous Portal (1 quadrillion tokens/day claimed). Today OpenRouter added **`meta/muse-spark-1.2-contributor`**, Meta's Muse Spark contributor variant, which is now the newest model on the platform by creation timestamp, ahead of V4 Flash Vision Exp. ([x.com/opencode](https://x.com/opencode/status/2090544355824038300)) · ([x.com/NousResearch](https://x.com/NousResearch)) · ([openrouter.ai/meta/muse-spark-1.2-contributor](https://openrouter.ai/meta/muse-spark-1.2-contributor))

5. **Z.ai turns Build Week into 50k × 100M free GLM-5.3 tokens, and Liquid ships DSpark speculative decoding (Aug 20-21)** — Z.ai announced Build Week Round 2: 50,000 new ZCode users get 100M free GLM-5.3 tokens each (login window Aug 22 00:00–Aug 24 09:00 UTC+8). Separately, Liquid AI released **DSpark draft models** for LFM2.5-1.2B-Instruct / 2.6B / 8B-A1B, where a lightweight draft model proposes a block of tokens ahead of the large model for a big decoding speedup with minimal extra memory and no quality loss. ([x.com/Zai_org](https://x.com/Zai_org)) · ([x.com/liquidai](https://x.com/liquidai))
