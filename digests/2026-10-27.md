Successor to GPT-5.6 Terra; API pricing 50% below GPT-5.6 Terra / GPT-6.1 Sol. Pitched on strong cost-efficiency on Agents Last Exam, MMMU-Pro, and OSWorld 2.1. Available in API, ChatGPT Work, and Codex.

**3. OpenAI claims AI-produced breakthroughs on Kakeya, ETH, and 4D QFT — UNCONFIRMED, no peer review yet.**
Oct 26 post claims AI-produced results on the Kakeya conjecture, that the Exponential Time Hypothesis is false, and rigorous 4D QFT/QCD existence, with academic shepherds enlisted to verify. Treat as unconfirmed science until independent verification lands.
- [Building scientific understanding with academic communities](https://openai.com/index/building-scientific-understanding-with-academic-communities/)
- [Third-party summary of the three claims](https://shattered.io/openai-three-science-discoveries-academic-shepherds-2026/)

**4. Cloudflare discloses Workers AI cross-customer data leak via GLM-5.3 + SGLang KV-cache bug.**
155 responses affected Aug 27–Sep 9, 2026, with prompts >681K tokens potentially exposed. An undersized KV-cache pool triggered an SGLang disaggregated-mode bug. Fixed; no evidence of deliberate exploitation; coordinated disclosure Oct 26.
- [KV-cache exposure remediation](https://blog.cloudflare.com/kv-cache-exposure-remediation/)

**5. Meta launches ~$35B stock offering to fund AI expansion.**
Oct 26: ~$22.5B Class A forward sales + ~$12.5B mandatory convertible preferred + $5.25B greenshoe; separate planned $60B ATM program.
- [Reuters: Meta launches $35B offering](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/meta-launches-35-billion-stock-offering-fund-ai-expansion-2026-10-26/)
