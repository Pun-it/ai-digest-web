## 📄 New Research Papers

From arXiv `cs.CL | cs.AI | cs.LG | cs.CV` sorted by submittedDate descending (fetched 2026-08-20 11:35 UTC). All from Aug 19.

1. **SPADE: Self-Play in Adaptive Synthetic Executable Environments** — LLM plays both Environment Designer (writes Gym-style training envs as code) and Reasoning Agent; optimizing regret targets environments at capability edge while staying feasible. +5.3 avg over fixed-env baseline at 30B, +13.9 on ACEBench-Agent. Step toward open-ended self-improvement. ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19197)) — project: https://spade-rl.github.io

2. **Beyond Teacher Likelihood: Group-Calibrated On-Policy Distillation for Long-Context Reasoning (GC-OPD)** — Fixes teacher-verifier mismatch in long-context OPD via group-normalized residual + RACA credit assignment. Qwen3-4B/8B 5-benchmark avg 29.08→40.47 and 35.12→44.65. ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19181))

3. **Open-MOPD: Diagnosing and Fixing Capability Imbalance in Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation** — Standard multi-teacher OPD recovers only 35.6% of oracle headroom; token-share balancing + gap-aware budget + reward refresh lifts to 83.4% on SmolLM3-3B. Fully open recipe. ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19098))

4. **What is Missing from AI Post-Training AI: An Empirical Analysis** — Across many post-training trajectories, agents lock strategy at the start and only do local adjustments. Experience scaffold helps execution (+12.6 GSM8K, +40.8 HumanEval) but not strategy re-evaluation. Identifies missing mechanism for spontaneous strategy revision. ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19072))
