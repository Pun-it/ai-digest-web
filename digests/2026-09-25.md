## 🔥 Top Stories

**OpenAI launched GPT-6 Sol and Luna on Sep 22 — half the price, half the mistakes of the 5.6 generation, rolling out across ChatGPT, Codex, and the API.**
Sol targets complex coding and agentic workflows while Luna handles high-volume focused tasks (summarization, extraction, quick Q&A). Pricing is $2/$10 per M input/output tokens for Sol (vs $4/$20 for 5.6 Sol) and $0.10/$0.50 for Luna (vs $0.20/$1.20) — OpenAI credits caching and inference improvements. On an internal factuality eval built from real conversations where users flagged mistakes, GPT-6 Sol makes about half as many mistakes as its predecessor, reaching Astra-level reliability at lower cost.
Sources: https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/ · https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/openai-launches-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/ · https://thenewstack.io/openai-gpt-6-sol-luna-release/ · https://community.openai.com/t/announcing-gpt-6-sol-and-gpt-6-luna-in-the-api-codex-and-chatgpt/1399925

**Anthropic fired first the same day: Claude Opus 5.5 launched Sep 22, just ~90 minutes before OpenAI's announcement, matching Fable 5.1-class performance at 40% lower run cost than Opus 5.**
Anthropic positions it as leading in agentic coding and knowledge work, with gains in coding, agentic and computer-use tasks, math/science reasoning, and long-horizon professional work, plus lower token prices and faster output. System card and platform docs are live.
Sources: https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5 · https://www.reuters.com/business/anthropic-unveils-claude-opus-55-2026-09-22/ · https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/opus-5-5/overview · https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5-system-card

**xAI released Grok 4.7 on Sep 21 — its most capable model for coding and knowledge work, with 500K context at the same price and speed as Grok 4.6.**
xAI says it works longer on difficult tasks, checks its own work more carefully, and ships its best-calibrated safeguards to date. Artificial Analysis scores Grok 4.7 (xhigh) at 46 on the Intelligence Index — though it burns ~81K output tokens per task, more than double Grok 4.6's 38K. A Grok 4.7 Fast variant at 2x token rates is exclusive to Cursor and Grok Build.
Sources: https://x.ai/news/grok-4-7 · https://docs.x.ai/developers/grok-4-7 · https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/benchmarking-grok-4-7 · https://cellcog.ai/blog/grok-4-7-release-date/

**Xiaomi open-sourced the MiMo-V2.6 series on Sep 22 — three omnimodal open-weight models, with MiMo-V2.6-Pro taking the #1 open-model spot on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at ~46.**
Expanded RL compute pushed MiMo-V2.6-Pro past Kimi K3 and Qwen3.8 Max (AA Intelligence Index v4.3, Sep 2026). OpenCode made MiMo V2.6 Flash free for a week and carries both Flash and Pro in Go.

## 📄 New Research Papers

Fresh submissions (Sep 24, 2026) via arXiv, categories cs.AI / cs.LG / cs.CL / cs.CV:

- **LLM Agents Can Easily Tamper With Their Own Traces** — async monitoring and audits assume agents can't alter execution traces; this work shows local agents can. Directly relevant to agent-deployment safety. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30266
- **PoEM: Predicting RL Outcomes from Existing Policies** (Hamidieh, Daras, Torralba) — predict post-training RL results without running full RL, cutting post-training cost/instability. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30226
- **Minimally Invasive Steering of Language Models** — regularized pre-logit steering toward test-time rewards without degrading generation quality. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30218
- **VeriSpeak: Retrieval-Augmented Fact Checking in Speech** — verify claims directly from spoken audio (news clips, podcasts, speeches) instead of transcripts. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30227
- **RAPID: Robot Agentic Programming from Demonstrations** (Mao et al.) — coding agents that auto-generate, verify, and refine robot programs from demos. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30249
- **Rolling-WAM: World Action Models with Rolling Imagination** — cuts joint video-action denoising latency in robotic manipulation replanning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30247
- **ExplorationBench: Measuring AI Systems' Exploration in Verifiable Alien Worlds** — evaluates hypothesis-framing and experiment design in novel simulated worlds. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30199
- **Agentic Detection of Online Conspiracies** — handles endorsement vs. satire vs. criticism ambiguity in conspiratorial discourse. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30250
- **Temporal Gradient Inversion for Private Trajectory Reconstruction in Embodied RL** — shows policy gradients alone can leak full sensor trajectories; a privacy warning for distributed robot learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30258
- **Towards Practical Compression of 3D Gaussian Splatting** — simpler storage compression for 3DGS novel-view synthesis. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30245
