## 🔥 Top Stories

**Riemann Hypothesis proved with Claude — Lean FRO and Mathlib Initiative say they have verified it.**
Levent Alpöge, a mathematician employed at Anthropic, posted a note and Lean formalization on Nov 20 claiming a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis and its generalized form, developed with Claude over about six weeks. On Saturday afternoon (Nov 21) the Lean Focused Research Organization and the Mathlib Initiative announced they had independently checked the formalization: "We have full confidence that Alpöge's proof of the Riemann hypothesis is correct." The timing — one day after Anthropic's Nasdaq debut — drew its own scrutiny, which Alpöge acknowledged ("The precise timing is horrible"). Caveat: Lean verification certifies the formal proof as stated; the broader mathematical reception and peer review are still developing.
- [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/21/anthropic-riemann-hypothesis-solved-claude-ai) · [Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/anthropic-mathematician-claims-to-prove-riemann-hypothesis-most-famous-problem-in-all-of-math/)

**Anthropic's IPO closed with the underwriters' option fully exercised — 1.127B Class A shares at $83.00.**
The company announced the closing on Nov 20, including all 147,000,000 option shares and a concurrent private placement; Dario Amodei rang the Nasdaq opening bell on Nov 19 as the listing went live. It was a record-breaking debut, and it landed the day before the Riemann announcement.
- [Anthropic newsroom (closing announcement)](https://www.anthropic.com/news) · [Guardian IPO takeaways](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/21/anthropic-riemann-hypothesis-solved-claude-ai)

**OpenRouter off-boarded three inference providers and pulled 125 endpoints from Zero Data Retention after Dragos found months of prompt theft.**
Dragos seeded roughly two million prompts with unique canary links and credentials between July and November, then watched for anyone using them; attackers inside three providers copied real customer traffic alongside the bait, including requests flagged ZDR. One attacker planted a couple hundred AWS keys and drove tens of thousands of AWS API calls; a Chinese lab fetched secret links from more than half the seeded prompts sent to its models, including about two-thirds of ZDR-marked requests. OpenRouter says its own systems were not breached. Separately, Together AI confirmed an AI coding agent leaked a Hugging Face credential in its xorl project, exposing stored June–July prompt samples.
- [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/openrouter-drops-3-providers-after.html) · [OpenRouter incident announcement](https://openrouter.ai/announcements) · [Dragos canary research coverage](https://www.unite.ai/openrouter-cuts-three-providers-after-ai-prompt-exposure-findings/)

**Architect Labs unveils Frostbite: an AI-discovered CPU architecture claiming 4.59× performance-per-watt over a modern superscalar core.**
Frostbite is a general-purpose CPU with a new instruction set built around interaction nets — every step local, every dependency explicit, every value single-owner. The company says its AI systems autonomously iterated over compiler, ISA, architecture, microarchitecture, RTL, verification and final GDS, then measured the result on 25 workloads (15 held out until final evaluation) against a silicon-proven dual-issue in-order core. The result is a company claim pending independent replication.
- [Architect Labs: Introducing Frostbite](https://architectlabs.com/blog/frostbite)

**Thinking Machines releases Inkling 2 Mini — a 26.4B/3.4B-active looped open-weights model that runs on a laptop.**
The middle 12 of 24 layers run three times per token (48 layer applications), which buys reasoning depth without longer chains of thought. Apache 2.0, ~20GB footprint, most weights trained and deployed natively in NVFP4 with the last four layers in MXFP8, 1M-token context, and hosted on Tinker. Thinking Machines reports 48.2% on DeepSWE v1.1 and 46.5% on Vals Index v2.2 — best among open-weights models under 100B total parameters — and Artificial Analysis scores it 20 on the Intelligence Index, calling it Pareto-optimal for intelligence vs. active parameters. It is #1 trending on Hugging Face today.
- [Thinking Machines announcement](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-mini/) · [Model card](https://huggingface.co/thinkingmachines/Inkling-2-Mini) · [AA analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/inkling-2-mini)

## 🚀 Frontier Model Releases

Hy4 Flash is a 1M-context multimodal model that became an unexpected hit as a free endpoint on OpenCode; it is currently #4 trending on Hugging Face overall.

## 📄 New Research Papers

*arXiv's newest announced batch is Nov 19 (no weekend announcements).*

- **Beam Model Technical Report** — 501.3B-total / 23.4B-active sparse MoE built specifically for coding, reasoning and agentic workloads. [arXiv:2611.26358](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26358)
- **Depth Pro 2: Sharp and Temporally Consistent Metric Monocular Depth** — full-resolution dense metric depth for images and video, with temporal consistency. [arXiv:2611.26603](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26603)
- **AgentRoboCritic: Understanding the Capability of Frontier Agents on Direct Robot Control** — evaluates agents like GPT-6 Astra driving robots via low-level actions without high-level primitives. [arXiv:2611.26613](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26613)
- **RL Post-Training in Diffusion Transformers Is Activation Steering** — argues RL post-training gains in diffusion transformers reduce to activation steering, a useful mechanistic handle. [arXiv:2611.26627](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26627)
- **SoK: Assessing the Evolution of LLM Capability in Software Vulnerability Research** — systematizes what LLM agents can actually do across discovering, validating and patching real vulnerabilities. [arXiv:2611.26601](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26601)
- **PGX: Physically Grounded Execution for Long-Horizon Humanoid Loco-Manipulation** — targets long-horizon VLA tasks where temporal structure breaks single-skill policies. [arXiv:2611.26596](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26596)
- **TideCache: Efficient Hybrid LLM Serving with Transparent Cache Management and Layer-Wise Streaming** — cache management for hybrid attention/state models, whose heterogeneous caches are a real serving bottleneck. [arXiv:2611.26619](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26619)
- **ScaleWorld: High-Resolution World Generation through Shared Prediction and Refinement** — high-res video world models without full-resolution generation cost. [arXiv:2611.26615](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26615)
- **AutoMaster: Autonomous Post-Training by Making the Same Model a Better Teacher** — self-teaching post-training loop that filters unsupported teacher trajectories. [arXiv:2611.26510](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26510)
- **Represented but misread: Post-training changes how language models read out what they represent** — probes vs. model answers suggest post-training alters readout more than representation. [arXiv:2611.26584](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26584)

Also worth reading: OpenAI's **MedChemBench** (Nov 20) — 99 problems drawn from 117 papers and patents and 761 molecules. GPT-6.2 Astra earned 84.5% of available credit, but compound selection and property prediction were much harder than reading structures. OpenAI is explicit that this does not show models can discover drugs. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-medchembench/)
